The conditions you can start with

Every one plugs into the health systems: blood, infection, pain, medication, sleep and Vitality.
Multiple Sclerosis-16
Heat brings stiffness, clumsy hands and pain.
Arthritis-12
Stiff joints slow movement and swings.
Heart Condition-12
Strain builds into chest pain; push through it and your heart gives out.
Asthma-10
Cold air, corpses, exertion and panic can bring on an attack.
Diabetes (Type 1)-10
Needs insulin to bring blood sugar down.
Hemophilia-10
Bleeding never stops on its own.
Migraines-10
Hours of pain, nausea and blurred vision.
Robbed-10
Robbed, shot and left for dead.
Wounded-10
Starts with a broken left arm and four fresh wounds, one of them deep.
A Really Bad Day-8
Starts naked, drunk, soaked, sick, with an infected cut.
Brittle-8
Hard hits can break an arm, hand or leg.
Germaphobe-8
Stressed and unhappy when dirty or bloody.
Gout-8
Rich food, drink and thirst build urate; past the line a flare in one foot: days of severe pain, slow walking, no running.
Hunted-8
They followed you here. A horde reaches the house a few hours in and everything nearby comes with it; a helicopter that afternoon; a smaller pack at dawn for three days, wherever you are.
Major Depressive Disorder-8
Depressive episodes hold mood down for days.
Sensory Issues-8
Scratchy clothes, grime, loud noise, bright sun, bad smells and crowds add up: lots of stress, then a meltdown or a shutdown.
Alcoholic-6
Needs a drink every day.
Epilepsy-6
Tiredness, stress, illness and drink can bring on seizures.
High Strung-6
Every fight stays with you.
Diabetes (Type 2)-5
Blood sugar runs high, worse when overweight.
Smoker-5
Cravings build with the habit; going without brings irritability, stress, hunger and restless sleep.
Anaemic-4
Tires sooner, recovers slower, catches colds easier.
Can't Switch Off-4
Burnout eases 0.67x as fast.
Gluten Intolerance-4
Wheat brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
Vegetarian-4
Refuses meat, fish and insects.
Essential Tremor-3
Shaky hands: rough stitches, slips, slower reloading and reading.
Exercise Averse-3
Hates working out: training brings boredom and low mood instead of a lift.
Injury Prone-3
Twice as likely to pull a muscle, and a pull takes longer to heal.
Lingering Soreness-3
Muscles take much longer to recover after training.
Straight Edge-3
Refuses alcohol and tobacco.
Underactive Thyroid-3
Without levothyroxine: tires faster, a low mood, the cold bites, weight comes on.
ADHD-2
Boredom builds 50% faster, skill books are a slog, and now and then you drift off mid-task.
Caffeine Dependent-2
Headache, tiredness and low mood without coffee or tea.
Carpal Tunnel-2
Long runs of the same hand work bring numb, tingling hands: slower, clumsier fine work, and they wake you at night.
Hallucinations-2
Sees and hears things that aren't there.
Hay Fever-2
March to August: sneezing fits that interrupt you and carry to zombies, worst on dry windy days outdoors.
Homebody-2
Home does you twice the good.
Inflexible-2
Stretching does half as much, and stiffness lingers.
Sensitive Skin-2
Damp, musty or unwashed clothes itch, and so does dryer-sheet perfume.
Sweaty-2
Clothes go stale nearly twice as fast.
Tinnitus-2
Gunfire leaves you hard of hearing for a while.
Lactose Intolerance-1
Dairy brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
