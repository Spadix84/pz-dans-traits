The conditions you can start with

Every one plugs into the health systems: blood, infection, pain, medication, sleep and Vitality.
Multiple Sclerosis-16
Heat brings stiffness, clumsy hands and pain. Monthly flares bring days of weakness.
Arthritis-12
Stiff joints slow movement and swings. Cold and damp bring painful flares.
Heart Condition-12
Strain builds into chest pain; push through it and your heart gives out. Beta blockers halve the risk, nitroglycerin ends the pain.
Asthma-10
Cold air, corpses, exertion and panic can bring on an attack. Rest in clean, warm air or use an inhaler.
Diabetes (Type 1)-10
Needs insulin to bring blood sugar down. Low or high sugar brings shakes, nausea and harm.
Hemophilia-10
Bleeding never stops on its own. Loses far more blood. Bandages slow it, stitches stop a deep wound.
Migraines-10
Hours of pain, nausea and blurred vision. Triggers differ for everyone.
A Really Bad Day-8
Starts naked, drunk, soaked, sick, with an infected cut. If indoors, the house is on fire.
Brittle-8
Hard hits can break an arm, hand or leg.
Germaphobe-8
Stressed and unhappy when dirty or bloody. Wounds infect slightly less often.
Major Depressive Disorder-8
Depressive episodes hold mood down for days. Exercise, fresh air and comfort food help.
Sensory Issues-8
Scratchy clothes, grime, loud noise, bright sun, bad smells and crowds add up: lots of stress, then a meltdown or a shutdown. Two kinds of safe food and two you can't stand.
Epilepsy-6
Tiredness, stress, illness and drink can bring on seizures. Anticonvulsants make them rarer. Starts with a bottle.
Diabetes (Type 2)-5
Blood sugar runs high, worse when overweight. Exercise, weight loss and metformin help.
Alcoholic-4
Needs a drink every day. Withdrawal brings stress, pain and the shakes, then seizures for heavy drinkers.
Anaemic-4
Tires sooner, recovers slower, catches colds easier. Needs meat, fish, greens, eggs or iron pills.
Gluten Intolerance-4
Wheat brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
Vegetarian-4
Refuses meat, fish and insects. Eggs and dairy are fine.
Exercise Averse-3
Hates working out: training brings boredom and low mood instead of a lift. Sticking to a training plan pays half as much.
Injury Prone-3
Twice as likely to pull a muscle, and a pull takes longer to heal.
Lingering Soreness-3
Muscles take much longer to recover after training.
ADHD-2
Boredom builds 50% faster, skill books are a slog, and now and then you drift off mid-task. Stick with a job for 20 minutes and you hyperfocus: quicker work, more experience, no boredom.
Caffeine Dependent-2
Headache, tiredness and low mood without coffee or tea. A week without breaks the habit.
Hallucinations-2
Sees and hears things that aren't there. Sudden bouts of panic.
Homebody-2
Home does you twice the good. A day away and you miss it, badly.
Inflexible-2
Stretching does half as much, and stiffness lingers. Stiff joints ache more.
Sensitive Skin-2
Damp, musty or unwashed clothes itch, and so does dryer-sheet perfume.
Sweaty-2
Clothes go stale nearly twice as fast.
Tinnitus-2
Gunfire leaves you hard of hearing for a while.
Lactose Intolerance-1
Dairy brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
Straight Edge-1
Refuses alcohol and tobacco.
