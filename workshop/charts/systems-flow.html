Vitality Project: how the systems fit together

Everything on the left happens to every character. The systems in the middle turn it into effects. Traits plug into the systems (see the trait chart).
What happens to you
Systems, for everyone
What you see
Food and drinkWhat you eat, drink and smoke: fresh or junk, wheat, dairy, meat, carbs, iron, caffeine, alcohol.
EffortThe body's metabolic rate: idle, walking, fighting, chopping, sprinting. Training in the exercise menu.
Sleep and the light in the roomHours slept, how dark it was, how often you woke, how rested you got up.
Wounds, hits and fallsScratches, cuts, bites, shards and bullets; knocks to the head, falls, car crashes.
Weather, sun and heatAir temperature, body temperature, rain, humidity, cloud, the time of day, storms coming.
PillsThe mod's medications and vanilla's: beta blockers, painkillers, sleeping tablets, antibiotics, caffeine pills.
VitalityDiet, exercise and sleep in one slow score. Fit and Thriving lift endurance recovery, mood, healing and infection resistance; Run Down and Sluggish drag them.Also feeds: Spoons (the night's score), Heart Condition, how fast infections, concussions and wounds clear.
Sleep and the night's scoreThe light on your square sets how deep you sleep and whether it wakes you. Each night is scored: hours, waking, light, fever.Also feeds: Vitality, Spoons, Migraines, Depression, Epilepsy, Hallucinations.
BloodBleeding drains blood, not health, by wound, place and dressing. Pale, light-headed, shock and passing out, bleeding out. Volume returns in a day, red cells in a week.Also feeds: Fear of Blood, Anaemic, Hemophilia, Good Clotter, Fast Recovery, endurance recovery.
Wound infection and antibioticsAn hourly roll per wound by depth, dressing and dirt. Incubates unseen, climbs, stops healing, brings fever, then sepsis. Antibiotics are a course with a level in the blood.Also feeds: the night's score, Migraines, Hallucinations, MS flares, Asthma, blood sugar, Epilepsy.
Wound care and what First Aid tells youBandages wear out, unstitched wounds heal slowly and reopen, stitches tear; stitching and splinting are First Aid rolls. The health panel is written for the examiner's level.Also feeds: Infection (open or spent dressings), Blood (bandages, stitches), pain.
Concussion and passing outFalls, crashes and head hits: headache, nausea, dizzy falls, knockouts, a headache that comes back days later. Shock and faints put you on the floor in real time.Also feeds: Migraines, Hallucinations, Epilepsy, light waking you.
PainEvery lasting ache (hangover, migraine, withdrawal, gut flares, MS heat, concussion) is held on one pain stat. Drink dulls it by how drunk you are; painkillers blank it for a while.Also feeds: Depression (mood), Spoons (spent faster), Arthritis slips.
MedicationOne list of drugs: a level in the blood, a build-up over days for the daily ones, wearing-off notices, side effects now and then, overdoses. A Pill Caddy for the belt.Also feeds: Heart, Epilepsy, Diabetes, MS, Migraines, Asthma, Depression, Smoker, Anaemic.
Alcohol, hangovers and caffeineRelief follows the Drunk moodle, not the sip. A hangover waits for you to sober up or wake. Caffeine counts by what went into the mug.Also feeds: Alcoholic, Hollow Legs, Straight Edge, Caffeine Dependent, Migraines, Epilepsy, Heart, Spoons (a coffee hides the wall).
Sunburn and dehydrationBare skin in the midday sun burns in about three hours and hurts for a day; sun block keeps it off. Hours of thirst build a headache and tiredness.Also feeds: Migraines, Epilepsy, the night's score, Outdoorsman.
Spoons (Multiple Sclerosis)An energy budget refilled by the night and spent by effort, heat, pain, hunger and flares. Run out and you hit the wall; push on and you borrow from tomorrow.Also feeds: MS legs, grip and stamina; a flare.
Age20s, 30s or 40s. Shades red cells, concussion recovery, hangovers, Type 2 resistance, Brittle, Arthritis and Gym Regular.Set by the two age traits; sandbox options.
MoodlesEvery lasting effect has one (Moodle Framework). Three have a good side: beta blockers, anticonvulsants and sun block working.
The health panelWounds, dressings, stitches, infections, breaks, sunburn and concussion, in the words your First Aid level allows.
Notices over the headEvery turn for the worse or better is said once, ten seconds over the head, and published as a story event for other mods.
The stats you already watchEndurance recovery, pain, mood, stress, panic, tiredness, thirst, sickness, and carry weight.
