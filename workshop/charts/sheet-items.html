The pharmacy and what to carry it in

Medicine you have to find, ration and keep on you. Found in pharmacies, cabinets, hospitals and bathrooms.
Amantadine
MS fatigue pill. Treats: multiple sclerosis fatigue.
Anticonvulsants
Daily epilepsy pill. Treats: epilepsy.
Baclofen
Muscle relaxant. Treats: muscle stiffness and spasms from multiple sclerosis, including stiffness brought on by heat.
Clotting Powder
Styptic powder for a bleeding wound. Use: right-click the bleeding part in the health panel, dressing off.
Diazepam
Anti-anxiety pill. Treats: panic and anxiety.
Glucose Meter
Reads blood sugar from a drop of blood. Needs test strips.
Rescue Inhaler
Rescue inhaler. Treats: asthma attacks and airway irritation.
Living With Type 1
Health magazine. Teaches the exact insulin dose for any food, at any First Aid level.
Insulin Pen
Rapid-acting insulin. Treats: diabetes (Type 1 needs it).
Iron Pills
Iron supplement. Treats: anaemia.
Metformin
Daily diabetes pill. Treats: Type 2 diabetes.
Nicotine Gum
Nicotine gum. Treats: cigarette cravings (Smoker).
Nitroglycerin
Heart rescue tablet. Treats: chest pain (angina).
Pill Caddy
Weekly pill organiser. Holds medication only.
Prednisone
Steroid for flares. Treats: multiple sclerosis flares, arthritis flares.
Sumatriptan
Migraine pill. Treats: migraine attacks (ordinary painkillers barely touch them).
Sun Block
Rub it in and the sun can't burn bare skin for 8 hours. Does nothing for a burn you already have. 8 coats.
Test Strips
For a glucose meter. One strip per reading. 25 strips.
