The perks that pay for them

Some fold a vanilla trait in; most lean on the same health systems the conditions do.
Renaissance Faire Geek+10
+1 Spear, Long Blade, Axe and Blacksmithing. Every summer at the faire turns out to have been training.
Fast Recovery+8
Includes Fast Healer. Recovers lost blood faster.
Grit+8
Feels pain a third less.
Deep Sleeper+6
Needs less sleep. Light rarely wakes you.
Steady Hands+5
Includes Dexterous. Faster, safer stitching and splinting. Faster reloading and weapon swaps.
Good Clotter+3
Bleeding stops sooner. Loses less blood.
Thick Skull+2
Fewer, milder concussions. Faster concussion recovery. Shorter knockouts and faints.
Early Riser+1
Starts well rested. Sleep is a little more restful.
Gym Regular+1
Every exercise starts at half regularity, more in the 20s.
Hollow Legs+1
Gets drunk slower. Milder hangovers.
Meal Prepper+1
Starts on a good diet. Diet variety counts over five days.
Night Shift+1
Sleeps soundly during the day.
