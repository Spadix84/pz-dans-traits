The perks that pay for them

Some fold a vanilla trait in; most lean on the same health systems the conditions do.
Renaissance Faire Geek+10
+1 Spear, Long Blade, Axe and Blacksmithing. Every summer at the faire turns out to have been training.
Fast Recovery+8
Includes Fast Healer. Recovers lost blood faster.
Grit+8
Feels pain a third less.
Deep Sleeper+6
Needs less sleep. Light rarely wakes you.
Steady Hands+5
Includes Dexterous. Faster, safer stitching and splinting.
Homemaker+4
Washes clothes cleaner by hand, wearing them less. Loves clothes dried on the line.
Weightlifter+4
Barbell, dumbbell and improvised weights give a fifth more experience. Lifting a log or a battery is no riskier than a proper weight.
Well Conditioned+4
Muscles recover half as fast again and are ready to train sooner. A pulled muscle heals twice as fast.
Cool Customer+3
Barely breaks a sweat. Clothes stay fresh twice as long.
Good Clotter+3
Bleeding stops sooner. Loses less blood.
Natural Runner+3
Jogging is easy on the legs and counts double toward training plans and a workout. Running gives a quarter more Fitness experience.
Nomad+3
No place is home and none is missed. Sleeps as well under the sky as in a bed.
Regimented+3
Sticking to a training plan pays twice as much. One missed day a week does not break the plan.
Slob+2
Stale or musty clothes are no bother. Fresh ones are no joy either.
Thick Skull+2
Fewer, milder concussions. Faster concussion recovery.
Early Riser+1
Starts well rested. Sleep is a little more restful.
Endorphin Junkie+1
A workout lifts you twice as much, for twice as long. Two days without one and you get restless.
Gym Regular+1
Every exercise starts at half regularity, more in the 20s.
Hollow Legs+1
Gets drunk slower. Milder hangovers.
Meal Prepper+1
Starts on a good diet. Diet variety counts over five days.
Night Shift+1
Sleeps soundly during the day.
