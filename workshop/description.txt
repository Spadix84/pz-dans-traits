[h1]Vitality Project[/h1]
[b]Your survivor brings their body with them.[/b] Their age, their health, their habits and how well they look after themselves all shape how they get through the apocalypse. Build 42, singleplayer.

[b]Age[/b] is the frame you pick first, [b]Vitality[/b] is how well you keep that body up, [b]traits[/b] are what you brought with you, and [b]the health systems[/b] are what the world does to you: blood that runs out, wounds that go bad, concussions, medicine you have to find. They read each other: a bad night can set off a migraine, a fever pushes a diabetic's sugar up, and a fifty-year-old heals slower than a twenty-year-old.

[h2]Vitality: look after yourself and it pays[/h2]
Diet, exercise and sleep roll into one slow score that takes about three days to move a tier. Fresh, varied food, training and a dark, unbroken night lift it; junk food, idleness and bad nights drag it down.
[list]
[*][b]Fit and Thriving:[/b] faster stamina recovery and healing, better mood, and resistance to colds and wound infection.
[*][b]Thriving adds[/b] a kilo of carry weight and 1.5x Fitness and Strength experience.
[*][b]Sluggish and Run Down:[/b] the reverse, but it never drains health.
[/list]

[h2]Age: pick a decade first[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]20s (+6):[/b] stamina, healing and training all come faster. No bonus level.
[*][b]30s (0):[/b] the game as it is.
[*][b]40s (-2):[/b] +2 levels in your occupation's main skill, +1 in the others. You recover, heal and train a little slower.
[*][b]50s (-6):[/b] +3 and +2. Slower still, and hangovers hit harder.
[/list]
Each decade has traits of its own, and Strong, Athletic, Fit and the daily habits cost more as you get older.

[h2]Daily life[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Training.[/b] Arms, chest, core and legs tire and recover on their own. Train a rested group and it pays more; train it sore and you can pull a muscle. Sets are counted in reps, a log or a propane tank will do for weights, stretching helps, and training plans pay a bonus while you stick to them.
[*][b]Cooking.[/b] A proper cooked meal leaves an afterglow that eases low mood and stress for hours, more for a better cook and a richer dish, and fuels your training: protein for recovery, carbs for stamina, fat against hunger.
[*][b]Sleep.[/b] Sleeping dressed means shallower sleep (warm clothes are free in the cold), and Undress and Sleep strips you off and dresses you again in one click. A hot water bottle eases sore muscles and warms a cold bed.
[*][b]Home.[/b] Where you sleep most nights becomes home: boredom, low mood and stress ease there, nights there score better and books go faster. Two days away and you miss it.
[*][b]Laundry.[/b] Hand washing only gets clothes so clean, damp clothes go musty, sweat makes them stale and old blood sets in. Dry them by the fire or on a clothesline. Fresh clothes lift your mood.
[*][b]Reading for pleasure.[/b] Novels, comics and magazines have genres and real pages, and the longer you read the more each page eases stress and low mood. Each character has a favourite genre, and a finished book gives a Good Read bonus.
[/list]
[h2]Traits that change how you play[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Multiple Sclerosis (-16):[/b] you get a day's energy in spoons, set by last night's sleep and spent faster in the heat. Run out and you hit the wall; push on and you borrow from tomorrow. Monthly flares, and three 1993 medicines.
[*][b]Type 1 Diabetes (-10):[/b] a hidden blood sugar, a meter and insulin pens. Your First Aid level tells you what a meal will take: a rough guess at 3, the exact dose at 9. A bad low can knock you out.
[*][b]Heart Condition (-12):[/b] effort, panic, a heavy pack and the cold all strain the heart. Chest pain stops you running; push on and it gives out, and the recovery takes a week. Beta blockers and nitroglycerin help.
[*][b]Hemophilia (-10):[/b] bleeds never stop on their own. Bandage everything, stitch the deep ones, carry clotting powder. A scratch costs you a day on the sofa.
[*][b]Migraines (-10):[/b] each character has three personal triggers, worked out as you play. Sumatriptan cuts an attack short.
[*][b]Sensory Issues (-8):[/b] scratchy clothes, noise, crowds, glare and smells build a sensory load that ends in a meltdown or a shutdown. Quiet, the dark, a fidget toy and headphones bring it down.
[*][b]Crippled starts:[/b] A Really Bad Day (-8), drunk, sick, cut, naked, house on fire; Wounded (-10), broken arm, four wounds, one bandage; Robbed (-10), shot, naked, house emptied; Hunted (-8), a horde follows.
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Mid-save?[/b] Safe to add. An existing character is put in their 30s and keeps their skills.
[*][b]Point signs?[/b] Minus means a flaw and plus a perk, as on this page. The creation screen shows the sign the other way round.
[*][b]Too hard?[/b] Most systems have a switch on one of the five Vitality sandbox pages, and the conditions are traits you choose.
[*][b]A wound still takes health?[/b] As in vanilla, an open wound hurts its body part until bandaged (deep ones until stitched), most of all on the neck. Turn off Wound Damage (Vitality: Health) and wounds cost only blood.
[*][b]Other mods?[/b] Take A Bath And Shower, Project A-Life, Bandits and This Is Your Life are supported. Dynamic Traits and Expanded Moodles loads, but it doubles up on addictions, so pick one of the two.
[*][b]Bugs?[/b] Leave a comment, or open an issue on GitHub. Your console.txt helps.
[/list]
