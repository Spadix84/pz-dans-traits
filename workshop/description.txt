[h1]Vitality Project[/h1]
Realistic, balanced traits, mostly negative, that add complications to work around, plus a Vitality system and a health overhaul for every character. Build 42, singleplayer only.

[h2]For everyone[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Vitality:[/b] diet, exercise and sleep roll into one slow score. Good food, training and good nights lift endurance recovery, mood, healing and infection resistance; junk and neglect drag them.
[*][b]Sleep and light:[/b] the light on your square sets how deep you sleep. Dark nights score better; a lit room rests you less and can wake you. Close the curtains.
[*][b]Blood:[/b] bleeding drains blood, not health, by wound, place and dressing. Pale at 15% lost, light-headed at 30%, shock at 40% (health draining, fainting), dead at half. Volume returns in a day; red cells take a week.
[*][b]Wound infection:[/b] every hour a wound may take one, likelier deep, dirty, open or with a shard in. It incubates unseen, climbs, stops the wound healing, brings fever, then sepsis. Disinfectant pushes it back early; antibiotics are a course, not one magic pill.
[*][b]Wound care:[/b] bandages wear out, unstitched deep wounds heal slowly and reopen, fresh stitches tear under strain, and stitching and splinting are First Aid rolls that can go wrong.
[*][b]What First Aid tells you:[/b] the health panel is written for the examiner's level: plain words at 0 to 2, the detail from 3, hidden infections at 9.
[*][b]Concussion and passing out:[/b] falls, crashes and head hits concuss: headache, nausea, dizzy falls, knockouts, a headache that returns days later. Shock, a knockout or Fear of Blood puts you on the floor in real time, zombies still coming.
[*][b]Sunburn and dehydration:[/b] bare skin in the midday sun burns in about three hours and hurts for a day; sun block (new item) keeps it off. Hours of thirst build a headache and tiredness.
[*][b]Hangovers and drink relief:[/b] drink past a buzz and a hangover waits for you to sober up or wake. Alcohol dulls pain and panic by how drunk you are, not by the sip.
[*][b]Medication:[/b] every pill has a level in the blood, a build-up over days for the daily ones, a wearing-off notice, side effects now and then, and an overdose. Diazepam (new) for panic; a Pill Caddy (new) for the belt.
[*][b]Age:[/b] 20s, 30s or 40s shade healing, hangovers, red cells and concussions.
[/list]
Most systems have a switch on the Vitality Project sandbox page. Every lasting effect has a moodle.

[h2]Negative traits[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Multiple Sclerosis (-15):[/b] heat is the enemy: warm tires you, hot stiffens the hands and slips the weapon, overheated makes them give out. Flares about monthly. Your energy is a budget of spoons: a good night refills it, everything spends it, run out and you hit the wall and borrow from tomorrow. Three 1993 pills: prednisone, baclofen, amantadine.
[*][b]A Really Bad Day (-12):[/b] drunk, sick, a shard wound, no clothes, house on fire. Stitch the wound; a needle and thread wait in a house nearby.
[*][b]Arthritis (-10):[/b] stiff joints, slower swings, flares in cold and damp; a grip that slips into a weak swing and, in a bad flare, can drop the weapon.
[*][b]Diabetes Type 1 (-10) / Type 2 (-5):[/b] a hidden blood sugar model; a moodle says out of range, the meter says which way. Insulin and strips, or metformin. A bad low puts you on the floor; high sugar feeds infections.
[*][b]Heart Condition (-10):[/b] chest pain while the Endurance moodle shows; push through it and a heart attack puts you down. Beta blockers, built up over days, cut both.
[*][b]Brittle (-8):[/b] solid hits can fracture a limb.
[*][b]Brittle Asthma (-8):[/b] cold air, corpses, exertion, panic and smoke irritate the airways through four tiers to an attack that drains you and coughs loud enough for zombies. Rescue inhaler.
[*][b]Hemophilia (-8):[/b] bleeds never stop on their own and reopen until bandaged; a bandage only slows one to two fifths. Stitches are what stop it.
[*][b]Major Depressive Disorder (-8):[/b] episodes hold mood down for days; drink, comfort food, exercise, the outdoors and a real antidepressant regimen all matter.
[*][b]Migraines (-8):[/b] attacks from bad sleep, thirst, stress, hangovers, caffeine withdrawal, fever, heat, corpses, storms and daylight: hours of pain and nausea. Three personal triggers hit twice as hard. Sumatriptan (new, rare) is what works.
[*][b]Epilepsy (-6):[/b] seizures, likelier tired, hungover, feverish, concussed, dehydrated or in a diabetic low: an aura, then down for minutes. Anticonvulsants cut them to a tenth.
[*][b]Fear of Blood (-6, reworked):[/b] first aid takes longer, and stitching, pulling glass or bullets, or dressing a bleeder can make you faint.
[*][b]Anaemic (-4):[/b] needs meat, fish, greens, eggs or iron pills; short of iron you tire, chill and rebuild blood slowly.
[*][b]Gluten Intolerance (-4):[/b] wheat brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
[*][b]Jinxed (-4):[/b] containers near you sometimes lose an item.
[*][b]Smoker (-4, reworked):[/b] cravings by habit, withdrawal that irritates and starves, lungs that cough and recover slowly. Anyone who smokes enough gains it; three clean weeks lose it.
[*][b]Vegetarian (-4):[/b] refuses meat, fish and anything cooked with them.
[*][b]Germaphobe (-3):[/b] dirty skin and clothes build stress; getting clean is a relief. Wounds a fifth less likely to infect.
[*][b]Alcoholic (-2):[/b] a day dry brings craving, poor sleep, pain, shakes, and for heavy habits hallucinations and seizures. Anyone who drinks too often gains it; a sober month loses it.
[*][b]Caffeine Dependent (-2):[/b] half a day without coffee or tea brings a headache and low mood; cola and chocolate only put it off.
[*][b]Hallucinations (-2):[/b] phantom zombies, sounds, thumps, whispers and panic, worse tired, stressed, feverish, concussed or in withdrawal.
[*][b]Tinnitus (-2):[/b] your own gunfire brings on Hard of Hearing for a while and lightens sleep.
[*][b]In Their 20s (-2):[/b] no bonus profession level; heals fast, mild hangovers.
[*][b]Lactose Intolerance (-1):[/b] dairy brings a mild gut flare.
[*][b]Straight Edge (-1):[/b] refuses alcohol and tobacco, and their relief.
[/list]

[h2]Positive traits[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Renaissance Faire Geek (+10):[/b] +1 Spear, Long Blade, Axe and Blacksmithing.
[*][b]Fast Recovery (+8):[/b] Fast Healer folded in; blood comes back half as fast again.
[*][b]Deep Sleeper (+6):[/b] Wakeful folded in; light rarely wakes you and the dark does more good.
[*][b]Steady Hands (+5):[/b] Dexterous folded in; stitches and splints go wrong half as often and take a quarter less time. Not while your hands shake.
[*][b]Good Clotter (+3):[/b] bleeds run down twice as fast and lose a quarter less blood.
[*][b]Thick Skull (+2):[/b] concussed half as often, less badly, heals faster.
[*][b]Outdoorsman (2, reworked):[/b] as vanilla, and burns half as fast.
[*][b]Cat's Eyes (1, re-costed):[/b] as vanilla; light wakes you more easily.
[*][b]In Their 40s (+1):[/b] an extra profession level; slower healing, worse hangovers, brittler bones.
[*][b]Early Riser, Meal Prepper, Gym Regular, Iron Stomach, Night Shift, Hollow Legs (+1 each):[/b] a head start on sleep, diet or exercise; rotten food does half the harm; daylight hardly wakes you; drink hits less and hangovers are milder.
[/list]

[b]Items:[/b] inhaler, insulin pen, glucose meter, test strips, iron pills, metformin, nicotine gum, anticonvulsants, diazepam, sumatriptan, prednisone, baclofen, amantadine, sun block, pill caddy.
