[h1]1.5.0: Age 2.0, burnout and the crippled starts[/h1]
Safe mid-save: you keep every level you have, a vanilla Smoker with a habit is switched to this mod's Smoker once with a notice, burnout starts at zero with your kills and days so far counted as hardening, and new sandbox options start at their defaults. Full notes: CHANGELOG.md on GitHub.

[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]What each age wants[/b] (sandbox Age Wants). 20s: three days in the same patch of map and you get [b]itchy feet[/b] until you set foot somewhere new; a night that ends before seven leaves you slow for an hour unless there is coffee. 40s: a morning coffee and a meal at the usual hour each cheer you. 50s: the same bed, a meal at yesterday's hour and a morning drink keep you right; three broken days put you [b]out of routine[/b]; the cold reaches your joints sooner.
[*][b]Five new conditions.[/b] [b]Gout (-8, 40s+)[/b]: rich food, drink and thirst build to a flare that takes a foot for days; allopurinol daily, colchicine for a flare. [b]Essential Tremor (-3)[/b]: shaky hands, worse on coffee, tired or cold; a drink steadies them for an hour. [b]Underactive Thyroid (-3, 40s+)[/b]: levothyroxine daily or you tire, feel low and cold and put on weight. [b]Hay Fever (-2)[/b]: March to August sneezing fits that zombies hear; antihistamines help and make you drowsy. [b]Carpal Tunnel (-2)[/b]: hours of the same hand work bring numb hands and bad nights. Four new bottles to find.
[*][b]Three traits for the 20s[/b] in Green's place: [b]Natural (+3)[/b] weapon skills, Aiming and Reloading train a quarter faster; [b]Hot-Headed (-3)[/b] cravings make you angrier for longer; [b]Restless (-3)[/b] the same patch of map weighs on you twice as fast. Green is retired.
[*][b]Vitality's own Smoker (-5).[/b] Habit, cravings, withdrawal, lungs and cough now belong to this mod's trait; the game's Smoker is hidden (sandbox Show Vanilla Smoker brings it back). Your age sets how much smoke is already on your lungs.
[*][b]Burnout.[/b] Fighting wears on you over days: kills, panic, wounds, illness and withdrawal build it; sleep, calm hours, a wash, fresh clothes, a good meal, a book, a workout, a drink and a smoke ease it, half again at home. It never makes you fight worse: it slows what fighting teaches, down to 15% when burnt out. Push to breaking point and you fall apart at the first quiet moment. You harden with kills and days survived. Depressive episodes, hallucinations, migraines, MS flares and seizures come up to twice as often when burnt out. New moodle: Burnout. Four traits: Level Headed (+8), Unwinds Easily (+4), High Strung (-6), Can't Switch Off (-4).
[*][b]A Guide tab in the character window[/b]: every system and how they fit, a page each with a picture, plus What's New shown once after each update.
[*][b]Wounded (-10):[/b] a fractured left upper arm, four fresh wounds and one bandage. [b]Robbed (-10):[/b] a bullet still in you, a soaking rag of a bandage, nothing on, nothing in your pockets, the house emptied; left alone you have about an hour. [b]Hunted (-8, singleplayer):[/b] around hour three 45 zombies come for the house you woke in, the helicopter at hour four, and a pack of 15 at dawn for three mornings. Leave.
[*][b]Newbie Gains (+2):[/b] Strength and Fitness experience +30% until each is three levels above where you started.
[*][b]Adrenaline auto-injector[/b] (rare, anyone): two hours of no tiredness and full stamina, paid back with interest, and a 1 in 4 heart attack per shot, worse for each shot within a day. New moodle: Adrenaline.
[*][b]SpiffBoy support[/b] (with the SpiffBoy console mod): play eases burnout like reading, played-out cartridges cheer less, screen time slows a concussion and hurts in a migraine. New [b]Vitality Cartridges[/b] mod: Still Water (breathing), Steady (a reflex test), Spiffo Walk, First Aid Quiz (teaches First Aid to 3) and Spiffo Pup.
[*][b]Wound Expansion support[/b] (sandbox Wound Expansion Compatibility, on): where both mods model the same thing this mod's version runs and Wound Expansion's stands down (its concussion, torn and poorly placed stitches, septic fever, necrosis and sickness from an infected wound, its own infection roll). Its dirty wounds spoil the dressing and make infection likelier here, torn bandages keep their halved life, and a blood bag puts blood back. Everything else of its own is left to it.
[*][b]Dan's Vanilla Fixes: rarity colours built in.[/b] Every item's name takes a rarity colour from the loot lists; Item Rarity UI is no longer needed. Sandbox Rarity Colours turns it off.
[*][b]Notes tab[/b] for Migraines (triggers found and still to find) and Sensory Issues (safe and can't-stand foods).
[/list]
[h2]Changed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Lighter on the frame.[/b] A player's profiler put Vitality's per-frame work top of the list while standing still. That path now asks the game far less for the same behaviour. If you profile mods: the game credits all of Vitality to DanTraits_Attrib.lua, so the OnPlayerUpdate row there is the whole mod.
[*][b]Age 2.0.[/b] In Their 40s now costs nothing; In Their 20s costs 5, starts two levels off the occupation's main skill and one off its others, and learns faster across the board (x1.15 everything, x1.3 under level 3, x1.5 Fitness and Strength). The young panic a little faster, pick up habits x1.3 faster, crave sooner and angrier, and have more depressive and psychotic episodes. Arthritis, Brittle, Type 2 Diabetes, Gout, Underactive Thyroid and Hard of Hearing are for the 30s and up; the creation screen hides them until you pick an age that can.
[*][b]The game's own traits are left alone.[/b] Cat's Eyes, Fear of Blood, Smoker, Iron Gut, Resilient, Keen Cook and Blacksmith Knowledge are no longer rewritten; what this mod adds to them is shown beside the cost ("+6 Vitality") and in the description. Other trait mods no longer clash here.
[*][b]Steady Hands, Fast Recovery and Deep Sleeper[/b] do their own work instead of handing you Dexterous, Fast Healer or Wakeful (taken back once). Each is exclusive with its twin.
[*][b]Dynamic Traits and Expanded Moodles compatibility[/b] (thanks chuchu): its duplicate traits are hidden and its in-play addictions become this mod's.
[*]A shorter health panel; Daily Medication moves to its own window behind a pill bottle button next to Fitness.
[*][b]Everyday activity is lighter on the muscles:[/b] running, carrying and fighting tire them a quarter as much, Sore at most, never a pull; only the fitness window can overwork or pull one, and only it breaks a plan's rest day.
[*]Alcoholic gives 6 (was 4), Smoker 5 (was 4), Straight Edge 3 (was 1).
[*]Fabric protector holds about five washes and keeps clothes fresh longer. Cyclobenzaprine is folded into baclofen, which now eases strains and a Bad Back for anyone.
[*][b]Experience bonuses and penalties multiply[/b] instead of adding, so a penalty can never take more than the gain.
[*]Split-screen co-op: the second player gets the damage and swing events, kits and age levels too. Base Unloader tells stacked crates apart.
[/list]
