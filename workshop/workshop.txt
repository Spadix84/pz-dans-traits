title=Vitality Project (Traits and Health Overhaul)
description=[h1]Vitality Project[/h1]
description=Realistic, balanced traits, mostly negative, that add complications to work around, plus a Vitality system and a health overhaul for every character. Build 42, singleplayer only.
description=
description=[h2]For everyone[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Vitality:[/b] diet, exercise and sleep in one slow score. Good food, training and good nights lift endurance recovery, mood, healing and infection resistance; junk and neglect drag them.
description=[*][b]Sleep and light:[/b] the light on your square sets how deep you sleep. Dark nights score better; a lit room rests you less and can wake you.
description=[*][b]Blood:[/b] bleeding drains blood, not health. Pale at 15% lost, light-headed at 30%, shock at 40%, dead at half. Volume returns in a day, red cells in a week.
description=[*][b]Wound infection:[/b] an hourly roll per wound by depth, dressing and dirt. Incubates unseen, climbs, stops healing, brings fever, then sepsis. Antibiotics are a course, not one pill.
description=[*][b]Wound care:[/b] bandages wear out, unstitched deep wounds heal slowly and reopen, stitches tear under strain. Stitching and splinting are First Aid rolls.
description=[*][b]What First Aid tells you:[/b] the health panel is written for the examiner's level: plain words at 0 to 2, detail from 3, hidden infections at 9.
description=[*][b]Concussion and passing out:[/b] falls, crashes and head hits concuss; a headache can return days later. Shock, a knockout or Fear of Blood puts you on the floor in real time.
description=[*][b]Sunburn and dehydration:[/b] bare skin burns in about three hours of midday sun and hurts for a day; sun block keeps it off. Hours of thirst bring a headache and tiredness.
description=[*][b]Hangovers and drink relief:[/b] drink past a buzz and a hangover waits for you to sober up or wake. Alcohol dulls pain and panic by how drunk you are.
description=[*][b]Medication:[/b] every pill has a level in the blood, a build-up for the daily ones, a wearing-off notice, side effects now and then, and an overdose. Diazepam (new) for panic; a Pill Caddy (new) for the belt.
description=[*][b]Age:[/b] 20s to 50s. The young recover, heal and train faster; the old know more.
description=[/list]
description=Most systems have a switch on the Vitality Project sandbox page. Every lasting effect has a moodle.
description=
description=[h2]Negative traits[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Multiple Sclerosis (-15)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Heat is the enemy. Warm tires you; hot stiffens the hands and slips the weapon; overheated makes them give out. Cool off and it clears.
description=[*]Flares about monthly: stiff legs, weak hands, exhaustion for days.
description=[*]Spoons: a day's energy after a good night, fewer after a bad one. Everything spends them, faster in heat or a flare. Run out and you hit the wall; push on and you borrow from tomorrow. Rest gives a little back.
description=[*]Prednisone shortens a flare, baclofen eases the stiffness, amantadine adds spoons. -1 Fitness and Strength; not with Athletic or Strong.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]A Really Bad Day (-12):[/b] drunk, sick, a shard wound, no clothes, house on fire. Stitch the wound: a needle and thread wait in a house nearby.
description=[*][b]Arthritis (-10)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Stiff joints, slower swings, flares in the cold and damp.
description=[*]A grip that slips into a weak swing; in a bad flare it can drop the weapon (sandbox option).
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Diabetes Type 1 (-10) and Type 2 (-5)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]A hidden blood sugar model. The moodle says out of range; the meter says which way.
description=[*]Type 1: insulin pen and strips. Type 2: metformin and weight.
description=[*]A bad low puts you on the floor; high sugar feeds infections.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Heart Condition (-10)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Chest pain while the Endurance moodle shows. Push through it and a heart attack puts you down.
description=[*]Beta blockers, built up over days, cut both.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Brittle (-8):[/b] solid hits can fracture a limb.
description=[*][b]Brittle Asthma (-8)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Cold air, corpses, exertion, panic and smoke irritate the airways through four tiers.
description=[*]An attack drains you and coughs loud enough for zombies. The inhaler ends it.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Hemophilia (-10):[/b] bleeds never stop on their own and reopen until bandaged. A bandage only slows one to two fifths; stitches stop it.
description=[*][b]Major Depressive Disorder (-8):[/b] episodes hold mood down for days. Drink, comfort food, exercise, the outdoors and a real antidepressant regimen all matter.
description=[*][b]Migraines (-10)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Attacks from bad sleep, thirst, stress, hangovers, caffeine withdrawal, fever, heat, corpses, storms and daylight: hours of pain and nausea.
description=[*]Three personal triggers hit twice as hard.
description=[*]Painkillers barely help. Sumatriptan (new, rare) halves an attack in the aura or ends it within two hours.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Epilepsy (-6)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Seizures, likelier tired, hungover, feverish, concussed, dehydrated or in a diabetic low. An aura, then down for minutes.
description=[*]Anticonvulsants, built up over five days, cut them to a tenth.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Fear of Blood (-6, reworked):[/b] first aid takes longer. Stitching, pulling glass or bullets, or dressing a bleeder can make you faint; so can bleeding fast.
description=[*][b]Anaemic (-4):[/b] needs meat, fish, greens, eggs or iron pills; short of iron you tire, chill and rebuild blood slowly.
description=[*][b]Gluten Intolerance (-4):[/b] wheat brings cramps, nausea and low mood.
description=[*][b]Jinxed (-4):[/b] containers near you sometimes lose an item.
description=[*][b]Smoker (-4, reworked)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Cravings by habit, withdrawal that irritates and starves, lungs that cough and recover slowly.
description=[*]Anyone who smokes enough gains it; three clean weeks lose it.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Vegetarian (-4):[/b] refuses meat, fish and anything cooked with them.
description=[*][b]Germaphobe (-3):[/b] dirty skin and clothes build stress; getting clean is a relief. Wounds infect a fifth less.
description=[*][b]Alcoholic (-2)[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]A day dry brings craving, poor sleep, pain and shakes; heavy habits get hallucinations and seizures.
description=[*]Anyone who drinks too often gains it; a sober month loses it.
description=[/list]
description=[*][b]Caffeine Dependent (-2):[/b] half a day without coffee or tea brings a headache and low mood; cola and chocolate only put it off.
description=[*][b]Hallucinations (-2):[/b] phantom zombies, sounds, whispers and panic, worse tired, stressed, feverish, concussed or in withdrawal.
description=[*][b]Tinnitus (-2):[/b] your own gunfire brings on Hard of Hearing for a while and lightens sleep.
description=[*][b]In Their 40s (-2), 50s (-4):[/b] 2 or 3 extra profession levels; slower to recover, heal and train.
description=[*][b]Lactose Intolerance (-1):[/b] dairy brings a mild gut flare.
description=[*][b]Straight Edge (-1):[/b] refuses alcohol and tobacco, and their relief.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Positive traits[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Renaissance Faire Geek (+10):[/b] +1 Spear, Long Blade, Axe and Blacksmithing.
description=[*][b]Fast Recovery (+8):[/b] Fast Healer folded in; blood comes back half as fast again.
description=[*][b]Deep Sleeper (+6):[/b] Wakeful folded in; light rarely wakes you and the dark does more good.
description=[*][b]Steady Hands (+5):[/b] Dexterous folded in; stitches and splints go wrong half as often and take less time. Not while your hands shake.
description=[*][b]Good Clotter (+3):[/b] bleeds run down twice as fast and lose a quarter less blood.
description=[*][b]Thick Skull (+2):[/b] concussed half as often, less badly, heals faster.
description=[*][b]Outdoorsman (2, reworked):[/b] as vanilla, and burns half as fast.
description=[*][b]Cat's Eyes (1, re-costed):[/b] as vanilla; light wakes you more easily.
description=[*][b]In Their 20s (+3):[/b] recovers, heals and trains faster; no extra profession level.
description=[*][b]Early Riser, Meal Prepper, Gym Regular, Iron Stomach, Night Shift, Hollow Legs (+1 each):[/b] a head start on sleep, diet or exercise; rotten food does half the harm; daylight hardly wakes you; drink hits less and hangovers are milder.
description=[/list]
