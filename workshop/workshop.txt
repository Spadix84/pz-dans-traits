description=[h2]Traits[/h2]
description=Negative: Alcoholic, Brittle, Arthritis, Jinxed, Caffeine Dependent, Migraines, Hemophilia, Anaemic, Major Depressive Disorder, A Really Bad Day, Hallucinations, Brittle Asthma, Gluten Intolerance, Vegetarian, Diabetes Type 1 and Type 2, Heart Condition, Epilepsy, Multiple Sclerosis, Tinnitus, Lactose Intolerance, Germaphobe, Straight Edge.
description=
description=Positive: Renaissance Faire Geek, Gym Regular, Iron Stomach, Early Riser, Meal Prepper, Deep Sleeper, Steady Hands, Night Shift, Hollow Legs, Fast Recovery, Good Clotter, Thick Skull.
description=
description=Reworked vanilla: Smoker, Fear of Blood, Cat's Eyes, Outdoorsman. Age: In Their 20s and In Their 40s.
description=
description=[h2]For everyone[/h2]
description=Vitality (diet, exercise and sleep), sleep and light, hangovers, drink relief, sunburn (and sun block), dehydration, blood loss and shock, wound infection and antibiotics, wound care, what First Aid tells you, concussion and passing out. Every lasting effect has a moodle. Most systems have a switch on the Vitality Project sandbox page.
