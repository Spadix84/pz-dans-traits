[h1]Dan's Weapons[/h1]
[b]Five weapons to make from what is already in the house.[/b] A hockey stick, a bread knife, the cutlery drawer, a padlock and a pair of socks: things you walk past on day one. Each has its own model and icon, needs no skill and no recipe to learn, and takes little more than duct tape. They give you an edge in the first days. They are not a replacement for a real axe.

[url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits/master/workshop/weapons/lineup.png][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits/master/workshop/weapons/lineup.png[/img][/url]

[h2]The weapons[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Hockey Stick Cleaver[/b] (Axe, two hands). A meat cleaver taped and wired to the far end of an ice hockey stick. It cuts like a hand axe at nearly a hockey stick's reach: damage 0.7 to 1.4, reach 1.45. It can be sharpened.
[*][b]Bread Knife Glaive[/b] (Long Blade, two hands). A bread knife taped along the end of a long handle, a broom or a mop. Damage 0.9 to 1.6 and reach 1.5, the longest of the five.
[*][b]Cutlery Mace[/b] (Short Blunt, one hand). Three forks and three butter knives taped round the head of a handle or a rolling pin. Damage 0.6 to 1.1, about a nightstick, and it draws blood.
[*][b]Lock in a Sock[/b] (Short Blunt, one hand). A padlock in a long sock. Damage 0.5 to 1.0, about a hammer. It is quick and light, hits one target a swing and wears out fast.
[*][b]Sock Flail[/b] (Short Blunt, one hand). The lock in a sock taped over the end of a small handle. Damage 0.7 to 1.3, with more reach and a heavier knock.
[/list]
For scale, the game's hand axe does 0.7 to 1.5 at reach 1.1 and its machete 2.0 to 3.0.

[h2]Making them[/h2]
All five are in the crafting menu under Weaponry from the start.

[url=https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits/master/workshop/weapons/recipes.png][img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits/master/workshop/weapons/recipes.png[/img][/url]

[list]
[*][b]Hockey Stick Cleaver:[/b] an ice hockey stick, a meat cleaver, 2 duct tape and a wire or electric wire, with pliers, on any surface.
[*][b]Bread Knife Glaive:[/b] a bread knife, a long handle (or a broom or a mop) and 2 duct tape, on any surface.
[*][b]Cutlery Mace:[/b] a handle (or a rolling pin), 3 forks, 3 butter knives and 3 duct tape, on any surface.
[*][b]Lock in a Sock:[/b] a pair of long or heavy socks you are not wearing, and a padlock. Made in your hands with no tools.
[*][b]Sock Flail:[/b] a lock in a sock, a small handle and 1 duct tape. Made in your hands.
[/list]
The game's three handle sizes (long handle, handle, small handle) are each a way into one.

[h2]Fragile, but nothing is wasted[/h2]
[list]
[*]They break sooner than real weapons. When one does, the useful part drops at your feet: the cleaver, the bread knife, the padlock, or a fork and a butter knife from the mace.
[*]The cleaver, the glaive and the lock in a sock can be taken apart again for their parts. The tape is lost.
[*]Wear carries over. A worn stick, pole or handle makes a worn weapon, and so does a worn cleaver or knife. The two long ones have two conditions, like the game's knife spears: the shaft and the blade.
[*]All but the lock in a sock can be repaired with tape, and the cleaver, the glaive and the mace with glue as well.
[/list]

[h2]Good to know[/h2]
[list]
[*]Build 42.13 or later. No sandbox options and no other mod needed. Safe to add to a running save. If you remove it, the weapons you made go with it.
[*]Nothing is added to the loot tables: you make them. None of them chops trees.
[*][b]The sock is stiff.[/b] A weapon model in the game is one rigid shape, so the sock cannot hang and swing. It is modelled the way it is mid-swing, pulled out straight by the padlock, which looks right while it is moving and a little odd standing still.
[*]Multiplayer: not yet tested, so please report anything odd.
[/list]
