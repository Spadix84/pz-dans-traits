[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Grit (+8)[/b] (new trait): you feel pain a third less. Painkillers work as before.
[*][b]Resilient can beat the Knox virus.[/b] A 25% chance (sandbox [i]Resilient: Knox Survival Chance[/i]): you get sick like anyone else, and if you're lucky the fever breaks partway through. Every infection rolls again. Resilient now costs 10.
[*][b]Knowing your insulin.[/b] With Type 1 diabetes, First Aid now tells you how much insulin a food takes: from level 3 a food's or a sugary drink's tooltip says whether its sugar hits fast or slowly and gives a rough range of doses, from 6 a closer range (and the glucose meter says how much insulin is still working), from 9 the exact number (and the meter says what it takes to get back to normal). New magazine [b]Living With Type 1[/b] teaches the exact dosing at any level: hospitals, medical offices, pharmacies, bookstores and libraries. The insulin pen can now give 3 doses at once. A food that would spike you past 350 even with its insulin tells you to eat it in parts. The glucose meter's tooltip remembers its last reading.
[*][b]Clotting Powder[/b] (new item, 5 uses): right-click a bleeding part in the health panel, dressing off. A scratch or cut stops bleeding; a deep wound bleeds at a quarter for 12 hours (still stitch it), and a bandage over it soaks far slower. Stings. Barely helps with glass or a bullet still in; a hemophiliac's bleed only halves. Medicine cabinets, first aid kits, medics' bags, pet shops, salons, army medical.
[/list]

[h2]Changed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Age, reworked.[/b] Four decades now, and you feel them every day. [b]In Their 20s (+6)[/b]: endurance comes back a quarter faster, Fitness and Strength train half as fast again, wounds heal and stiffness fades half as fast again; no bonus profession level. [b]In Their 40s (-2)[/b]: two levels in the profession's main skill and one in each of its other skills, a body a little slower at all of that. [b]In Their 50s (-6, new)[/b]: three in the main skill and two in the others, slower still, and everything age already made harsher is harsher again. The 30s are the game as it is. The 20s now cost points and the 40s give them (it was the other way round). Strong and Athletic cost 2 points more in the 40s and 4 more in the 50s, Stout and Fit 1 and 2; Fit, Fast Learner, Slow Learner and Hearty Appetite are priced by age too (green on the creation screen when cheaper). The mind and the metabolism go with the body: the young learn new skills faster and burn through food, drink and medication faster; the old keep learning their trade, and a daily pill holds a fifth longer in the 50s, as does a hangover. Everyone carries an Age trait, and the creation screen asks for it first: nothing else is offered until you have picked one (In Their 30s costs nothing). The Unemployed get their levels in Maintenance, and the creation screen shows age's levels in blue. Old saves keep their skills.
[*][b]Traits only one age can take.[/b] 20s: [b]Green (-4)[/b], new to the job, every occupation skill a level lower (XP gain is unchanged); [b]Quick Study (+4)[/b], skills under level 5 train x1.4. 40s and 50s: [b]Reading Glasses (-2)[/b], slow reading and no reading in dim light without them on; [b]Bad Back (-4)[/b], heavy loads hurt; [b]Bad Knees (-3)[/b], running and climbing hurt; [b]Old Hand (+4)[/b], your occupation's skills train a quarter faster. 50s: [b]Old Injury (-3)[/b], one limb that aches in the cold and damp; [b]Set in Their Ways (-2)[/b], skills outside your occupation train slower. Then traits of each band's own: 20s [b]Bottomless Pit (-6)[/b], always hungry; 40s [b]Pace Yourself (+2)[/b], a tenth of a swing's stamina back, and [b]Settled (-4)[/b], sleeps badly off a house bed; 40s and 50s [b]Delicate Stomach (-2)[/b], junk food and drink on an empty stomach disagree; 50s [b]Seen It All (+3)[/b], panic builds slower, [b]Cast Iron (+2)[/b], pills agree with you, and [b]Old Bones Know Rain (+1)[/b], tomorrow's weather felt today.
[*][b]Age traits, drawn.[/b] Every Age trait and every age-only trait now has its own icon.
[*][b]Shorter trait descriptions.[/b] Every trait's tooltip is rewritten in vanilla's style: a few short lines with the headline effects, no more walls of text.
[*][b]Arthritis flares, eased.[/b] The cold is now read from the joints' skin, so gloves and a coat keep it out. Painkillers halve a flare while they last; prednisone cuts it to a third. Neither touches the everyday stiffness, and a character on painkillers never throws the weapon. A new Arthritis character starts with a bottle of painkillers.
[*][b]Pill Caddy:[/b] uncommon now, not rare, and a found one often has a bottle or two in it.
[*][b]Sumatriptan[/b] turns up twice as often: nearly as often as the other prescriptions, and as likely as diazepam in a found Pill Caddy.
[*][b]Hemophilia, survivable.[/b] A bandage now slows a bleed to a quarter (it was two fifths), a soaked bandage is never worse than anyone else's open wound, and changing a bandage no longer starts the bleed over: the bleeding clock is remembered under the dressing, so a new bandage carries on where the old one got to. Keep a scratch covered and it can finally stop. Still -10, still no clotting.
[*][b]Balance pass.[/b] Alcoholic -4 (was -2), Settled -4 (was -2), Germaphobe -4 (was -3), Fast Recovery +6 (was +8). [b]Asthma[/b] (no longer "Brittle") -10 (was -8), and inhalers are half as common again (sandbox [i]Inhaler Loot[/i]).
[*][b]Iron Gut[/b] (4) takes in Iron Stomach: rotten and burnt food does half the harm and food sickness builds half as fast. [b]Thick Skull[/b] takes in Bounces Back: knockouts and faints are half as long. Characters with the old traits get the new ones.
[*][b]Keen Cook[/b] 4 (was 3), and adds +1 Short Blade.
[*][b]Jinxed[/b] is gone. Characters that had it lose it.
[*][b]A Really Bad Day[/b] -8 (was -12): the glass shard is now an infected cut on the forearm, so the start is survivable. Disinfectant and a bandage wait next door. Still not properly balanced.
[*][b]Steady Hands[/b] is quicker with a gun too: reloading and racking run a quarter faster (an ammo strap stacks), and a weapon goes to and from the belt a quarter faster. Not while your hands shake.
[/list]

[*]Knocked out (a concussion, shock, a seizure, a diabetic low), the black screen hid the pause menu too: Escape did nothing you could see. The menu, inventory and moodles now draw over the black, as they do while you sleep.
[*]The Pill Caddy's tab showed your whole carried weight ("12.3 / 1") while it hung on the belt, and Organized did not give it the second slot.
[*]In Their 20s did not hide Arthritis or Handy if the age was picked first. Exclusions now work in either order, for every trait.
[*]A drink no longer shows the Antidepressants moodle on a character without Major Depressive Disorder (the game gives a hidden antidepressant dose with every sip; the mod now undoes it like the beta-blocker and painkiller ones).

[*]In-game text that had drifted from what the code does: the Thriving moodle (Fitness and Strength train half as fast again, not twice), Hemophilia (stitches stop a deep wound too), Gym Regular (more in the 20s), Set in Their Ways (Fitness and Strength exempt), and the Starting Medication tooltip (now lists Multiple Sclerosis and Arthritis).

[h1]1.1.0: Multiple Sclerosis, medication, and the Reddit round[/h1]
Thanks to everyone who left ideas on r/projectzomboid. Safe to update mid-save. Full detail in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub: https://github.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits

[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Multiple Sclerosis (-15).[/b] Heat is the enemy: warm tires you, hot stiffens and hurts the hands and slips the weapon, overheated can make the hands give out. Flares about once a month. Three 1993 pills: prednisone shortens a flare, baclofen eases the stiffness, amantadine adds energy. Starts at -1 Fitness and -1 Strength; not with Athletic or Strong.
[*][b]Spoons.[/b] An MS character's energy is a budget: a day's spoons after a good night, fewer after a bad one. Everything spends them by effort, faster in the heat or a flare. Run out and you hit the wall; push on and you borrow from tomorrow. Rest gives a little back, a book costs next to nothing, a coffee hides the wall for an hour. Sandbox option [i]MS Spoons a Day[/i] (12; 0 turns it off).
[*][b]Shared medication system.[/b] Every pill in the mod and vanilla's now builds up, wears off (you're told when), has side effects now and then (sandbox [i]Medication Side Effect Chance[/i]), and can be overdone. [b]Diazepam[/b] (new) takes the panic drop that beta blockers used to give. 30-pill bottles. Sandbox [i]Starting Medication[/i]: start with your trait's pills or find your own.
[*][b]Pill Caddy[/b] (new, rare): a belt-worn organiser that holds medication only.
[*][b]Sumatriptan[/b] (new, rare): the migraine pill. In the aura, the attack is half as bad; in the attack, it's over within two hours. Once per attack; the day after you're heavy and a little clumsy (a Heavy-Limbed moodle shows it).
[*][b]Personal migraine triggers[/b] (three hit twice as hard, the rest half; your character works out which) and new triggers: heat, corpses nearby, a storm coming.
[*][b]Stitching is a First Aid roll.[/b] Rough stitches heal slower, tear easier, ache and let infection in. A suture needle or Steady Hands helps; from First Aid 3 you can tell, and the health panel shows it.
[*][b]Anger does something.[/b] The vanilla Angry moodle (a smoker's withdrawal raises it) now costs you from Annoyed up: melee weapons wear faster, reading is slower, splints, stitches and vehicle parts go wrong more often, and from Angry up you curse out loud where zombies can hear. Sandbox [i]Anger Has Effects[/i] turns it off.
[*][b]Antidepressants moodle:[/b] green while a pill is covering you (paler until the two-week regimen is at full effect), gone when you miss a day.
[*][b]Project A-Life compatibility:[/b] its NPCs and its zombie steering leave a Hallucinations phantom alone (no squad opening fire at something only you can see).
[*][b]The headache after a concussion[/b], a day or two later and worse than the knock. Sleep helps, daylight hurts.
[/list]

[h2]Changed[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Arthritis:[/b] a slipping grip makes a weak swing instead of throwing the weapon; only a bad flare can still drop it, and sandbox [i]Arthritis Drops Weapons[/i] turns that off.
[*][b]Migraines[/b] cost -10 (was -6); painkillers barely touch an attack now, sumatriptan is what works. The nausea is strong enough to show now, a lit room makes an attack worse and a dark one shortens it, a storm counts only in the twelve hours before it hits, an attack blurs your vision, sunglasses take the edge off the light, and a Light Too Bright moodle shows when the light is making it worse.
[*][b]A reason to get drunk:[/b] being drunk drains unhappiness, stress and boredom, faster the drunker you are. An evening blind drunk clears even a severe mood.
[*][b]Hangovers[/b] hit harder: two hours properly drunk is a full one and a small one is still felt, endurance comes back slower, daylight makes the headache worse, and painkillers only dull it (half as long).
[*][b]Hemophilia[/b] costs -10 (was -8).
[*][b]Smoker:[/b] cravings come half as often; sandbox [i]Nicotine Craving Moodle[/i] can hide the craving moodle.
[*][b]Caffeine Dependent:[/b] a brewed mug of coffee, tea or cocoa now counts (it never did); chocolate and cola put the craving off instead of being ignored.
[*][b]Alcoholic:[/b] tipsy ends the craving at a normal habit.
[*][b]Vitality:[/b] Thriving gives 1.5x Fitness and Strength XP (was 2x).
[/list]
