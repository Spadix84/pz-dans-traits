[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]ADHD (-2)[/b] (new trait): boredom builds 50% faster, skill books are a slog (reading one makes you bored), and now and then you drift off and stop what you're doing (never while panicking, never walking or climbing, not more than once an hour). But stick with a job for 20 minutes and you [b]hyperfocus[/b]: jobs go 15% quicker, you learn 10% more, boredom can't touch you and you won't drift off, until you stop for a few minutes. Coffee clears your head (less boredom, less drifting, locks in sooner), and walking into a building you've never been in is a thrill; home is a bit dull. Not with Homebody. New moodle: Hyperfocus.
[*][b]Sensory Issues (-8)[/b] (new trait): scratchy clothes, grime, loud noise, crowds of zombies, bright sun and bad smells all feed one sensory load. It brings lots of stress, then Overwhelmed, then an overload: a meltdown (panic and anger: you throw down what you hold and shout out loud) or a shutdown (no energy, no running, nothing matters). Overwhelmed, everything you do takes longer; overloaded, far longer. It lingers, and each overload makes the next come sooner until you sleep it off. Quiet, home, the dark and sleep bring it down (with Take A Bath And Shower, a bath three times as fast and a shower twice), and so does fidgeting with a toy or holding a plush. You start with two kinds of safe food and two you can't stand (crunchy, mushy, slimy, chewy, mixed, strong, plain, sweet), shown on the food's tooltip; overloaded, only safe food goes down, and a food eaten calmly often enough becomes safe. Ear muffs (cut the noise by 60%) and sunglasses help, and new [b]noise-cancelling headphones[/b] shut the noise out completely, at a price: worn, they leave you deaf to everything, zombies included. New moodle: Sensory Load.
[*][b]Fitness training.[/b] Your muscles tire and recover. Arms, chest, core and legs each carry what you put them through: train a group once it has recovered (a day or so later) and it pays a quarter more, train it sore and it pays far less and can pull a muscle (pain, stiffness, and that group is off limits for a day and a half). Sleep, good food, stretching and your age set how fast they recover. A 15-minute workout leaves you Pumped for three hours (stress, low mood and boredom ease); seven hard days in a row leave you Overtrained until you take two easy ones. New moodles: Sore Muscles, Pumped, Overtrained. Sandbox options turn it off, or just the pulled muscles.
[*][b]Sets in reps.[/b] The fitness window asks how many reps, not how many minutes, and the set ends on the last one (or when you are spent), with a progress bar. Every rep tires the muscles the same, so slow squats are no longer worse than quick push-ups. A sandbox option, Training Experience (%), sets how fast Fitness and Strength grow.
[*][b]New exercises.[/b] No gym? Curl a log, a propane tank, a full bucket or a car battery, or do one-arm curls and overhead presses with a paint can; the exercise is named for what you hold. The heavier the load, the more it builds. Stretching speeds recovery and leaves you Loosened Up (stiff joints ache less). Jogging, carrying a heavy load and hard work like chopping and digging count as training by themselves. A weight bench nearby is worth 15% more, and right-clicking one (or a treadmill) opens the fitness window.
[*][b]Training plans.[/b] Pick a plan in the fitness window: a seven-day cycle of targets (upper body, legs, jogging, stretching, rest...). Do each day's job and you are On Plan: experience and recovery are 15% better. Miss one and the bonus is gone for two days, then back; the window says when. Fitness Instructors know every plan. Overtraining takes real work: a hard day is 40 minutes plus 6 for each level of Fitness and Strength, seven in a row; jogging and carrying a heavy load count half toward it (and toward a rest day's limit), so a week of hauling loot home is not a week of overtraining. The window now shows each muscle group's state, today's target and the last seven days.
[*][b]Fitness magazines.[/b] Five new magazines in gym lockers, sports shelves and magazine racks. Each teaches a training plan and, once read, adds 5 to 10% to all your Strength or Fitness experience for good.
[*][b]Cooking pays off.[/b] A proper cooked meal (300 calories of a dish with two or more ingredients) now does more than fill you. Its [b]afterglow[/b] eases low mood and stress for three to five hours, on top of what the meal itself gives; a better cook, more ingredients and spices make it bigger, and so does eating at home. The same dish twice in a day does half as much. Dishes come in three tiers: [b]Home-cooked[/b], [b]Hearty[/b] (Cooking 3, three ingredients) and [b]Chef's[/b] (Cooking 7, four ingredients or three and a spice); the dish's tooltip says which it is for you. With Fitness Training on, a meal also leaves you [b]Fuelled[/b] for four hours (Fitness and Strength experience +5 to 15%), plus one buff by what the dish is mostly: [b]protein[/b] speeds muscle recovery, [b]carbs[/b] give back part of the stamina you spend, [b]fat[/b] keeps hunger off. Training while stuffed can give you a side stitch. A cooked meal counts as comfort food against depression for as long as it glows. New moodle: Good Meal. A sandbox option turns it off.
[*][b]Bulk and Cut.[/b] A diet drop-down in the fitness window. Bulk: +10% Strength experience while you are putting on weight. Cut: +10% Fitness experience while you are losing it, and the weight comes off a quarter faster. Eating well and training also nudges your weight back toward normal.
[*][b]Training bonuses have a ceiling.[/b] All of the mod's boosts on one Fitness or Strength gain together (Ready muscles, plans, a weight bench, traits, magazines, meals) now top out at double. The Training Experience (%) sandbox option still applies on top.
[*][b]Nine fitness traits.[/b] [b]Well Conditioned (+4)[/b]: muscles recover half as fast again, ready sooner, pulls heal twice as fast. [b]Weightlifter (+4)[/b]: weights give a fifth more experience, and a log or battery is no riskier than a real weight. [b]Natural Runner (+3)[/b]: jogging is easy on the legs and counts double; running gives a quarter more Fitness. [b]Regimented (+3)[/b]: twice the plan bonus, one missed day a week forgiven. [b]Endorphin Junkie (+1)[/b]: a workout lifts you twice as much for twice as long, but two days without one and you get Restless. [b]Lingering Soreness (-3)[/b]: muscles take much longer to recover. [b]Injury Prone (-3)[/b]: twice as likely to pull a muscle. [b]Exercise Averse (-3)[/b]: never Pumped, training bores and saddens you. [b]Inflexible (-2)[/b]: stretching does half as much and stiff joints ache more.
[*][b]Training and your other traits:[/b] a workout lowers blood sugar with diabetes (Type 1 and 2), during it and for hours after, so watch for lows. Burpees set off Asthma like running does. Heavy lifting and burpees strain a Heart Condition. A day on your legs helps Brittle bones. Improvised lifts are hard on a Bad Back, and a Bad Back pulls more easily in the core. A Gym Regular knows the Upper/Lower plan.
[*][b]A fit body eases Arthritis and MS.[/b] Their symptoms ease by up to a fifth at high Fitness and Strength while you keep training, and a tenth more after stretching. They never go away.
[*][b]Arthritis (-12, was -10) and Multiple Sclerosis (-16, was -15)[/b] can no longer start with Athletic, Fit, Strong or Stout. You can still earn them in play.
[*][b]Bad Back (-8, was -5)[/b] hurts more and stiffens the legs (you walk slower) under a heavy load. Push a sore back too far and you can throw it out: four hours of severe pain, a stiff back and no running. A trained core (sit-ups, burpees, carries) makes it build slower and go out less often. New moodle: Back Strain.
[*][b]Hot water bottles soothe sore muscles.[/b] Fill the game's hot water bottle with water and heat it on a stove, in a microwave or by a fire. It stays hot for an hour and warm for three (longer in bed, much less out in the frost); its tooltip says how long. Right-click it, [b]Hold on sore muscles[/b], and pick your arms, chest, core or legs: for half an hour that group recovers up to twice as fast, its stiffness fades, a pulled muscle there heals sooner, a Bad Back eases on the core and Arthritis aches less. Take a warm one to bed and all your muscles recover a little faster, and a cold night is a better one.
[*][b]Cyclobenzaprine[/b] (new item, rare): the muscle relaxant doctors handed out for a strain or a bad back. One tablet works for about 8 hours: stiffness fades twice as fast, and a pulled muscle or a Bad Back hurts and stiffens about half as much. It eases them; it doesn't heal them faster. It makes you drowsy with a dry mouth, far drowsier with a drink, and training on it gives less experience and pulls a muscle more easily. Too many at once and your heart races. Anyone can take it.
[*][b]Dryer sheets.[/b] A new box of 20, found in laundry rooms and on cleaning shelves. Right-click a dryer with the box on you to put a sheet in with the load: everything that comes out dry goes stale half as fast once worn, until its next wash. One sheet a load. [b]Sensitive Skin[/b] itches from the perfume.
[*][b]Insulin after the pharmacies are gone.[/b] Butcher a cow, pig, sheep or deer for its [b]pancreas[/b] (rabbits, raccoons and young animals give a small one), and make it into a 40-dose [b]insulin vial[/b], the same insulin as a pen: a fresh, raw pancreas (or four small ones), a sharp knife, vinegar, a clean rag and a cup of rubbing alcohol, vodka or whiskey, at First Aid 2. The recipe comes from a new magazine, [b]The Insulin Papers[/b] (as common as Living With Type 1 and in the same places, plus labs), and First Aid 8 knows it without one. A pancreas spoils in two days, so keep it cold. Vials also turn up in hospital and pharmacy storage and medical fridges, and you draw them up with an [b]insulin syringe[/b] (kept and reused; Type 1 now starts with one, and the Pill Caddy holds it). Pens are unchanged.
[*][b]Two diabetes sandbox options.[/b] [b]Insulin Loot (%)[/b] sets how often pens, vials and syringes spawn. [b]Untreated Diabetes: Days to Fatal[/b] sets how long blood sugar above 350 takes to kill: 1 (the default) is about a day as before, 14 a slow decline over two weeks, 0 never (health stops at 15%). Health above 350 now sinks gradually toward the end rather than holding at 15% and then dropping.
[*][b]Sleeping in your clothes.[/b] Going to bed dressed means shallower sleep: you rest up to 12% slower and the night scores worse, the more you have on. Underwear, socks, a T-shirt and shorts are fine. Warm clothes stop counting as it gets cold (free at 10C or below, and whenever you feel cold), so winter never asks you to strip and freeze; shoes, backpacks and armour always count. Deep Sleepers mind half as much. A sandbox option turns it off.
[*][b]Undress and Sleep.[/b] A new option right under Sleep on any bed (or sofa, or the floor): walk over, take off everything that costs you sleep, put your bags down by the bed and go to sleep, all in one click, no "are you sure?". The tooltip lists what comes off. When it's cold, warm clothes stay on (only shoes and gear come off). The clothes go into your inventory, and when you wake up you put them back on (whatever you still have on you; your bags stay by the bed). If the game won't let you sleep (zombies nearby, pain, panic), you get dressed again straight away.
[*][b]Medication reminder.[/b] On a daily medication for one of your conditions (beta blockers, anticonvulsants, metformin, baclofen, amantadine, antidepressants)? A new moodle tells you when the next dose is due ([b]Medication Due[/b], two hours early) and when you've let it wear off ([b]Missed Medication[/b]). The health panel has a new [b]Daily Medication[/b] section: when you last took each one and when the next is due. A sandbox option turns the moodle off.
[*][b]Blood on the health panel.[/b] A new [b]Blood[/b] section shows how much blood you have, in as much detail as your First Aid allows. With little or none you only see how you look: pale, light-headed, in shock, still bleeding. First Aid 3 names how bad the blood loss is, how fast you are bleeding and whether it is coming back or you need to drink. First Aid 6 gives the blood volume and red cells as rough percentages and how much you are losing an hour. First Aid 9 gives the exact figures and the time left: until shock, until it's fatal, or until you're back to full. Open another player's health panel and you see their blood the same way, by [i]your[/i] First Aid, in multiplayer too.
[/list]

[h1]1.3.0: Home, laundry and reading for pleasure[/h1]
Safe mid-save: home is worked out from the nights you sleep from here on, your clothes start clean and fresh, a half-read book keeps its place, and the sandbox settings you had carry over to the new pages. Every detail is in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub.

[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Home.[/b] Where you sleep most nights becomes home: three of the last seven nights in one place and it is yours, sleep somewhere new more often and home moves there, no claiming needed. At home, boredom, stress and low moods ease, nights slept there score better and books go faster (half as much at three nights, fully at seven). Two days away and you miss it, until you walk back in. New moodle: Home, with Settling In / At Home on the good side and Missing Home / Homesick on the bad. A sandbox option turns it off.
[*][b]Homebody (-2)[/b] (new trait): home does you twice the good, and a day away is missed, badly.
[*][b]Nomad (+3)[/b] (new trait): no place is home and none is missed; a night under the sky scores better.
[*][b]Laundry.[/b] Washing and drying take some care, on a sandbox page of its own (one switch turns it off). Hand washing gets clothes only so clean (40% without soap, 20% with); a new [b]washboard[/b] gets them cleaner and only a washing machine gets them spotless. Wrung clothes stay damp, and clothes left damp too long (worn damp, sooner) go musty. Worn clothes go stale with sweat, faster with hard work and heat. New moodle: Stale Clothes / Musty Clothes, a small dip in mood, and on its good side Fresh Clothes / Line-Dried when everything you wear is clean, dry and fresh (a small lift; twice that for clothes dried on a line in the sun). Wet clothes dry slower on your back and leave them a little stale, so it pays to dry them first. Old blood sets into a stain only bleach gets out, and washing slowly wears fabric. Dry clothes by the fire or on a [b]clothesline[/b] (the ones in yards work, and you can put one up with two sticks and two lengths of rope): sun, wind and warmth dry them fast, rain soaks them again. A worn poncho keeps the clothes under it clean (the garbage bag one wears with every splash of blood or dirt it takes and with hard work, until it tears apart, and there is a rough no-skill recipe for it), and new [b]fabric protector[/b] and [b]fabric wax[/b] halve the blood and dirt a garment takes. Right-click a washing machine to put every dirty garment you carry in it at once. Moved from Dan's Vanilla Fixes, which no longer touches washing.
[*][b]Five laundry traits.[/b] [b]Sweaty (-2)[/b]: clothes go stale nearly twice as fast. [b]Cool Customer (+3)[/b]: clothes stay fresh twice as long. [b]Slob (+2)[/b]: stale clothes are no bother, fresh ones no joy. [b]Homemaker (+4)[/b]: washes cleaner by hand with half the wear, and loves line-dried clothes. [b]Sensitive Skin (-2)[/b]: damp, musty or never-washed clothes itch.
[*][b]Laundry and your other traits:[/b] Nomad minds stale clothes half as much, a Homebody loves clothes dried at home, bleaching indoors sets off Asthma, washing bloody clothes stresses Fear of Blood, and a washboard is hard on a Bad Back.
[*][b]Germaphobe -8[/b] (was -4): hard to live with, now paid for.
[*][b]Reading for pleasure.[/b] Every novel, comic and magazine has a genre, real pages and a hidden quality. A novel takes hours, its comfort arrives page by page, and a book you put down keeps your place (right-click for Read a chapter). Each character likes one genre and dislikes another, found out by reading. Finishing a book gives the new [b]Good Read[/b] moodle: about two days of one bonus set by the genre, which the moodle names. Real books from before 1993 turn up among the game's own. Books do more good at home (twice that for a Homebody), less when concussed, a Bad Day's first day takes comfort from any book, and over 40 without reading glasses a page takes a little longer. Skill books and recipe magazines are untouched. A sandbox page of its own; one switch turns it off.
[*][b]Reading: lose yourself in it.[/b] The first few pages of a sitting take little off your stress and low mood; the longer you read without stopping, the more each page does, climbing over the first hour (sooner for a comic or magazine) to well above the old rate. Boredom goes page by page as before. Pick the same book straight back up after an interruption and you are still in it; a quarter of an hour away and you start cold.
[*][b]Sandbox pages:[/b] the options are split into Vitality: General, Health, Daily Life, Laundry and Reading. Saved settings carry over.
[/list]

[h1]1.2.0: Age, a heart that matters, and clotting powder[/h1]
The biggest update so far. Safe mid-save: an existing character becomes In Their 30s and keeps their skills, retired traits are swapped for their new homes, and starting kits are not handed out again. Every detail is in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub.

[h2]Headlines[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Age, reworked.[/b] Four decades, picked first on the creation screen. [b]20s (+6)[/b]: endurance back a quarter faster, Fitness, Strength, healing and new skills faster, no bonus level. [b]30s (0)[/b]: the game as it is. [b]40s (-2)[/b]: two levels in the job's main skill and one in its others, a body a little slower. [b]50s (-6, new)[/b]: three and two, slower still, and bleeds, concussions and hangovers hit harder. Strong, Athletic, Stout, Fit, Fast and Slow Learner and Hearty Appetite are priced by decade, and in the 20s the conditions a young body shrugs off (MS, Heart Condition, Asthma, Hemophilia, Type 2, Anaemic, Smoker, Alcoholic) give 1 to 3 fewer points.
[*][b]15 age-only traits.[/b] 20s: Green (-4), Quick Study (+4), Bottomless Pit (-6). 40s: Pace Yourself (+2), Settled (-4). 40s and 50s: Reading Glasses (-2), Bad Back (-4), Bad Knees (-3), Old Hand (+4), Delicate Stomach (-2). 50s: Old Injury (-3), Set in Their Ways (-2), Seen It All (+3), Cast Iron (+2), Old Bones Know Rain (+1). Each has its own icon.
[*][b]Heart Condition, rebuilt (-12).[/b] It never mattered in play. Now effort, panic, a heavy load and the cold build strain, and the Chest Pain moodle shows it coming. Chest pain means no running and dearer swings; push on and the heart gives out, which costs a week and leaves it weaker. A heart still mending gives out sooner, and a second attack within a day is nearly fatal. New [b]Nitroglycerin[/b] ends chest pain in a minute. Not with Athletic any more.
[*][b]Clotting Powder[/b] (new item): right-click a bleeding part in the health panel. A scratch or cut stops; a deep wound bleeds at a quarter for 12 hours (still stitch it). Found in medicine cabinets, first aid kits, pet shops and salons.
[*][b]Knowing your insulin.[/b] With Type 1, First Aid tells you what a food will do: a rough dose at 3, closer at 6, exact at 9. The new magazine [b]Living With Type 1[/b] teaches it at any level. The pen gives up to 3 doses at once.
[/list]

[h1]1.1.0: Multiple Sclerosis, medication, and the Reddit round[/h1]
Thanks to everyone who left ideas on r/projectzomboid. Safe to update mid-save. Full detail in CHANGELOG.md on GitHub: https://github.com/Spadix84/pz-dans-traits

[h2]New[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Multiple Sclerosis (-15).[/b] Heat is the enemy: warm tires you, hot stiffens and hurts the hands and slips the weapon, overheated can make the hands give out. Flares about once a month. Three 1993 pills: prednisone shortens a flare, baclofen eases the stiffness, amantadine adds energy. Starts at -1 Fitness and -1 Strength; not with Athletic or Strong.
[*][b]Spoons.[/b] An MS character's energy is a budget: a day's spoons after a good night, fewer after a bad one. Everything spends them by effort, faster in the heat or a flare. Run out and you hit the wall; push on and you borrow from tomorrow. Rest gives a little back, a book costs next to nothing, a coffee hides the wall for an hour. Sandbox option [i]MS Spoons a Day[/i] (12; 0 turns it off).
[*][b]Shared medication system.[/b] Every pill in the mod and vanilla's now builds up, wears off (you're told when), has side effects now and then (sandbox [i]Medication Side Effect Chance[/i]), and can be overdone. [b]Diazepam[/b] (new) takes the panic drop that beta blockers used to give. 30-pill bottles. Sandbox [i]Starting Medication[/i]: start with your trait's pills or find your own.
[*][b]Pill Caddy[/b] (new, rare): a belt-worn organiser that holds medication only.
[*][b]Sumatriptan[/b] (new, rare): the migraine pill. In the aura, the attack is half as bad; in the attack, it's over within two hours. Once per attack; the day after you're heavy and a little clumsy (a Heavy-Limbed moodle shows it).
[*][b]Personal migraine triggers[/b] (three hit twice as hard, the rest half; your character works out which) and new triggers: heat, corpses nearby, a storm coming.
[*][b]Stitching is a First Aid roll.[/b] Rough stitches heal slower, tear easier, ache and let infection in. A suture needle or Steady Hands helps; from First Aid 3 you can tell, and the health panel shows it.
[*][b]Anger does something.[/b] The vanilla Angry moodle (a smoker's withdrawal raises it) now costs you from Annoyed up: melee weapons wear faster, reading is slower, splints, stitches and vehicle parts go wrong more often, and from Angry up you curse out loud where zombies can hear. Sandbox [i]Anger Has Effects[/i] turns it off.
[*][b]Antidepressants moodle:[/b] green while a pill is covering you (paler until the two-week regimen is at full effect), gone when you miss a day.
[*][b]Project A-Life compatibility:[/b] its NPCs and its zombie steering leave a Hallucinations phantom alone (no squad opening fire at something only you can see).
[*][b]The headache after a concussion[/b], a day or two later and worse than the knock. Sleep helps, daylight hurts.
[/list]
