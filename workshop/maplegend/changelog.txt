[h1]1.1.0: for other mods[/h1]
[list]
[*][b]An API.[/b] MapLegend_API.lua and API.md in the mod folder: add a preset, read the entries and what a symbol and colour mean, set and unset a meaning, and a "changed" event. Loaded with pcall(require), so a mod runs with or without Map Legend.
[*][b]A page in the Vitality Project's Guide tab[/b] when that mod is loaded too: the legend, the editor and the one-legend-for-every-save rule.
[/list]
Safe mid-save: your legend is untouched.

[h1]1.0.0[/h1]
First release: a scrolling legend on the world map, a row for every symbol and colour you have used, a tooltip on each row, click a row to draw with it.
