# Project Zomboid Vitality Project

A Project Zomboid (Build 42) mod (internal id `DanTraits`, kept for save compatibility): realistic, balanced traits that add complications to work around, plus a Vitality system that makes fresh food, exercise and sleep matter for everyone.

### Negative traits

#### Multiple Sclerosis (-16)

Heat is the enemy, and no medicine helps with it. Your energy is a budget.

- **Heat load.** Follows the warm air (24 to 34 C) and your body temperature (exercise, too many clothes). Builds over about half an hour; fades over twenty minutes once cool, twice as fast wet. A drink straight from a tap, well or river takes some off; bottles do not.
- **Warm:** tire sooner, endurance comes back slower.
- **Hot:** stiff, sore, clumsy hands (the game's stiffness on hands and forearms). Swings can throw the weapon, 5% rising to 15%. Pain rises to about 60.
- **Overheated:** severe pain, and a 5% chance a minute the hands give out and drop what they hold. Not while asleep or in a vehicle.
- **Cooling off** clears it within about half an hour. MS takes back its own stiffness and pain.
- **Flares** about once a month, likelier with fever, stress or hours spent at the wall. Three to six days of the heat hitting half as hard again, stiff legs, weak hands and exhaustion.
- **Spoons.** You wake with a day's energy (12 spoons; sandbox option, 0 turns it off). The night sets the refill: hours, how rested you woke, waking, light, fever. A bad night gives fewer, never under a third. A flare caps it at eight.
- **Spending.** Every minute costs by effort (idle a spoon every two and a half hours, sprinting about three an hour), more in the heat, in a flare, in pain, panicking, hungry or uncomfortable. Reading or writing costs a fifth and counts as rest.
- **Running low.** At half, a warning. At three or fewer, stiff legs and slow stamina. At none, the wall: heavy tiredness, legs like a flare's, stamina at a crawl, a swing that can throw the weapon, and every hour you push on borrowed from tomorrow (up to six) with a likelier flare.
- **Getting some back.** Sitting still, not hungry, not laden, gives half a spoon an hour, up to two a day. A nap gives up to four. A coffee hides the wall for an hour, then it lands. You are told the count each morning.
- **Pills** (new items, on the [medication system](#medication)): prednisone makes a flare pass three times as fast from the first pill (hunger while you take it); baclofen builds up over two days to halve the stiffness (mild drowsiness); amantadine builds up over three days to add two spoons to every morning (dry mouth). Starts on baclofen and amantadine, built up, with a bottle of each.
- **A fit body helps a little.** Stiffness, pain and fatigue (the spoons spent) ease by up to 20% at Fitness and Strength 10 / 10 while the training is kept up, and 10% more while Loosened Up from stretching ([fitness](#fitness)). They never go away.
- **Also:** -1 Fitness and -1 Strength; not with Athletic, Fit, Strong or Stout at the start (earned in play as usual). Warms up a tenth faster than anyone else. Discomfort (the Uncomfortable moodle) adds stress and spends spoons half as fast again at full discomfort.
- **Moodles:** MS Heat, MS Flare, Spoons.

#### A Really Bad Day (-8)

A CDDA-style start.

- You begin drunk, sick, with an infected laceration on the left forearm, no clothes, and the house on fire.
- The cut is already infected and won't heal until it is treated: about a day and a half before it spreads into the body. Disinfectant pushes it back; once it has spread, only antibiotics do (see [Wound infection](#wound-infection)).
- Disinfectant and a bandage wait in a container in another house 15 to 40 tiles away. No marker; the trait text only says next door.
- Not with Hemophilia or Straight Edge.
- Not balanced yet; see Status and known issues.

#### Arthritis (-12)

Stiff joints and an unreliable grip.

- Slower to move and swing. A Stiff Joints moodle shows how much the weather is in the joints.
- Flares in the cold and damp (humidity, rain outdoors). The cold is read from the joints' own skin temperature, the game's thermoregulator, which clothing, wind and wet feed: gloves and a coat keep a flare off. Only with no reading does the air decide.
- Relief: painkillers halve the weather's share of a flare while they are in your system (about two hours a pill); prednisone cuts it to about a third (x0.35) while you take it. The stronger counts, they do not stack, and neither touches the everyday stiffness. A new character starts with a bottle of painkillers and a quarter bottle of prednisone, 5 pills (Starting Medication).
- A swing can slip: 1% calm, more when panicked, hurt, tired or flaring. A slipped swing lands at a third of the weapon's damage.
- Past half a flare one slip in three throws the weapon to the ground instead (a dosed full flare sits exactly at half, and is safe). The sandbox option Arthritis Drops Weapons turns that off.
- **A fit body helps a little.** The joints ease by up to 20% at Fitness and Strength 10 / 10 while the training is kept up, and 10% more while Loosened Up from stretching ([fitness](#fitness)). Inflexible makes them worse.
- Not with Athletic, Fit, Strong or Stout at the start (earned in play as usual).
- Cannot be taken in the 20s; flares sooner in the 40s.

#### Diabetes Type 1 (-10) and Type 2 (-5)

A hidden blood sugar model. You read it with a meter, not a number on screen.

- **Sugar** rises with the carbohydrates you eat and drink, and for Type 1 about 10 an hour with nothing on board. Insulin, exercise, alcohol, sleep and (above 180) the kidneys bring it down; insulin is the only treatment that does.
- **The moodle** (Blood Sugar) says the sugar is out of range, not which way. The glucose meter and a test strip say which.
- **Low:** shaky, tired, anxious, and a bad low puts you on the floor. Steady Hands stops working; Epilepsy seizures are likelier.
- **High:** thirst, and wound infections are likelier and climb faster. Over 350: nausea, and after two hours up there, health drains (a spike after a meal costs nothing; hours of it do).
- **Type 1:** no insulin of your own. Insulin pens and insulin vials (40 doses each) are the treatment; a vial needs an insulin syringe, kept and reused. Starts with three pens, a syringe, a meter and strips (the syringe, meter and strips even with Starting Medication off).
- **Animal insulin:** butchering a cow, pig, sheep or deer gives a Pancreas; piglets, lambs, fawns, rabbits and raccoons a Small Pancreas (rats, mice and birds none). Extract Insulin turns a fresh, raw one (or four small) into a vial, with a sharp knife, vinegar, a rag and 0.2 l of rubbing alcohol, vodka or whiskey, at First Aid 2. Taught by the magazine **The Insulin Papers**, or known at First Aid 8. A pancreas is fresh for two days.
- **Sandbox:** Insulin Loot (%) scales pens, vials and syringes. Untreated Diabetes: Days to Fatal (1 by default) is how long above 350 it takes for the health floor (15%) to sink to nothing; 0 keeps the floor for good.
- **Knowing your doses (Type 1):** one dose covers about 12.5 g of carbohydrate. With First Aid 3 a food's tooltip (or a sugary drink's, for the whole bottle or mug) says whether its sugar hits fast or slowly and gives a wide range of doses; First Aid 6 narrows the range, and the meter adds how much insulin is still working; First Aid 9 gives the exact doses for your body, and the meter says what it takes to get back to 110 (more doses, or grams of fast sugar), and a food that would spike you past 350 even with its doses says to eat it half, a third or a quarter at a time. The meter's tooltip keeps its last reading and advice. The magazine **Living With Type 1** (new item: hospital magazine racks, medical offices, pharmacies, bookstore and library medical shelves) teaches the First Aid 9 knowledge at any level.
- **Type 2:** the body's own insulin, limited by weight (and by age: more resistance in the 40s, a little less in the 20s). Metformin builds up over two days. Starts on it with a bottle, a meter and strips.

#### Heart Condition (-12)

A weak heart that is pushed too hard. Strain builds, chest pain follows, and a heart attack if you push through it.

- **Strain** (0 to 1) builds every minute from endurance spent, sprinting and running, how winded you are, panic from half way up, carrying more than you can and cold air; it drains at rest (from full to nothing in about 12 minutes standing still). Age (x0.8 / x1.25 / x1.5), a smoking habit, caffeine, a Run Down body, a stimulant overdose and every heart attack survived (+20% each, for good) make it build faster. Shown on the Chest Pain moodle from 0.4 (Heart Working Hard) and 0.75 (Heart Pounding).
- **Chest pain** at full strain: 15 to 30 minutes of pain, endurance recovery at a third, no running or sprinting (the Restricted Movement moodle on an attempt), swings cost half as much again. Twice as fast to pass at rest. Strain falls back to 0.8, so a second bout needs more pushing, not a fresh start.
- **Pushing on through it** (spending endurance at Endurance moodle 2 or worse, trying to run or sprint, or swinging) risks a heart attack each minute (8%, 4% on beta blockers): down for 5 to 15 minutes, 15 health lost, endurance emptied. A second attack within a day drops health to the floor.
- **The week after**, in three stages (Weak Heart moodle): endurance comes back at x0.5 / x0.65 / x0.8, swings, sprinting and running spend x1.5 / x1.3 / x1.15, melee weapons do x0.5 / x0.65 / x0.8 damage, and pushing on through chest pain is x2 / x1.5 / x1.25 as likely to bring another attack. You are told as each stage passes.
- **Beta blockers** (vanilla's pills, on the [medication system](#medication)), one a day, build up over three days to halve the strain build and the heart attack chance; while built up, endurance comes back a tenth slower (anyone on them). You are told when they wear off. Starts on them built up half way, with one bottle.
- **Nitroglycerin** (new item, 25 tablets, where prescriptions are and in a found Pill Caddy): one under the tongue ends chest pain within a minute and halves the strain, then half an hour of headache. Two within an hour drops your blood pressure: faints. Starts with a bottle.
- Not with Athletic (Fit is allowed).
- Moodles: Chest Pain (strain, pain, pushing; green while beta blockers work) and Weak Heart (the week after). Console: `heart`, `heart strain <0..1>`, `heart angina`, `heart attack`, `heart nitro`.

#### Brittle (-8)

- A solid hit (a weapon or a zombie, 2 damage or more) has a 20% chance to fracture one of six limbs.
- Falls and car crashes are judged the way Concussion judges them: nothing under its floor (a hop
  off a fence, a bump at 11 km/h), rising to 40% in a crash certain to concuss (a wall at 70 km/h
  is 40%, a fence at 50 about 22%, a second-floor drop about 7%).
- Likelier in the 40s.

#### Asthma (-10)

Airway irritation that builds through four tiers to an attack.

- **What irritates:** cold air, corpses nearby, exertion (spending endurance when it is already low), panic, smoke, and a wound infection's fever. A mask halves the environmental share.
- **Tier 1:** a warning. **Tier 2:** endurance recovers at half speed, the odd quiet cough. **Tier 3:** no recovery and coughing. **Tier 4, the attack:** endurance drains to empty and health to a 15% floor, you cough loud enough to pull zombies, and an attack that empties you can black you out.
- At rest, irritation eases over about four hours (twice as fast asleep) once the attack has ended; during one it barely eases on its own. The rescue inhaler (new item) ends it at once. Inhalers turn up half as often again as they used to; the sandbox option Inhaler Loot sets the rate.
- Smoking, and a smoker's lungs, make it worse.
- Bleaching clothes indoors irritates the airway too (0.08 a garment; a dust mask halves it, a gas mask stops it).
- Moodle: Airway Irritation.

#### Hemophilia (-10)

- Bleeds never run down on their own while unbandaged: the bleeding clock is held where it is. A bandage runs it down as usual, and the clock is remembered under the dressing, so changing a bandage carries on from where the old one got to instead of starting the bleed over. Open wounds bleed again until bandaged, unless a bandage already ran their clock to nothing.
- With [Blood](#blood) on: bleeds lose half as much again, and a bandage only slows one to a quarter. A soaked bandage, or one over a shard, is never worse than anyone else's open wound, so change it. Stitches stop a deep wound; a scratch stops only under a bandage.
- With Blood off: every unbandaged bleed costs extra health instead.
- Not with Good Clotter, Fast Recovery or A Really Bad Day.

#### Major Depressive Disorder (-8)

Episodes that hold mood down for days.

- Pain and stress drag mood; a bad night makes an episode likelier.
- An episode holds a mood floor by its severity, with a refractory window after.
- What lifts it: drink (briefly), cigarettes, comfort food, a piece of nicotine gum (a little), exercise, time outdoors, and a real antidepressant regimen.
- Antidepressants: a pill a day, a 3-day build-up, side effects, and discontinuation if you stop. Vanilla's instant lift is gone for this trait.
- Moodles: Depression, and Antidepressants (green while a pill is covering you, paler until the three days are up).

#### Migraines (-10)

Attacks that cost you hours.

- **Triggers:** bad sleep, thirst, stress, hangovers, caffeine withdrawal, fever, heat (hot air or an overheated body), corpses close by, a storm on the way (in the twelve hours before it is forecast to start), and bright daylight.
- **Personal triggers:** of the eight lifestyle ones, each character draws three that count double; the other five count half. You are not told which. Once a strong trigger has brought on two attacks, your character works it out and says so.
- **The attack:** an aura first, then hours of pain and nausea (Queasy untreated). Daylight outdoors adds the most pain and halves the recovery; a lit room adds some and slows it; a dark room speeds it. Sunglasses halve the pain the light adds. Your vision blurs for the attack (Short Sighted's blur, its shorter sight and worse aim with it). Sleep shortens it, less in a lit room. A refractory day follows.
- **Painkillers** barely touch an attack: a third of their usual relief, a tenth off the time.
- **Sumatriptan** (uncommon) is what works. Taken in the aura the attack is half as bad; taken during one it is over within two hours with the pain and nausea halved. Once per attack. The day after you are heavy and a little clumsy (a 3% grip slip a swing; moodle: Heavy-Limbed). Starts with a pack down to its last two tablets.
- Moodles: Migraine, Heavy-Limbed the day after sumatriptan (for anyone who takes it), and Light Too Bright during an attack (too bright, the light hurts, blinding: how much the light is adding; sunglasses take it down).

#### Epilepsy (-6)

- Seizures about one in eight days when rested and well. Tiredness, alcohol withdrawal, a hangover, fever, concussion, stress, dehydration, a diabetic low and bright sun make them likelier.
- An aura five to ten minutes ahead (never under 30 real seconds). Then you drop what you hold and are out for 2 to 5 minutes, which can concuss. Then an hour of headache and low mood.
- Asleep, a seizure wakes you and spoils the night instead.
- Anticonvulsants (new item, on the [medication system](#medication)), one every 12 hours, build up over five days to cut seizures to a tenth. You are told when they wear off. Starts on them, built up, with a bottle.
- Moodle: Seizure (the aura before, the hour after), with a good side while the pills work.

#### Fear of Blood (-6, vanilla reworked)

- Vanilla's panic at your own bleeding and stress when bloody stay.
- Treating a wound takes a quarter longer.
- Stitching (25%), pulling out a bullet (35%) or glass (20%), or dressing a bleeding wound (10%) can make you faint: about eight seconds, woken by anything that wounds you. So can losing blood fast.
- Still cannot perform first aid on others.
- Washing or bleaching a bloody garment is stressful (+5% stress each).

#### Anaemic (-4)

- Iron drains over about five days. Fresh meat, fish, greens, eggs and iron pills (new item) top it up.
- Short of iron: endurance recovers slower, you tire sooner and catch cold more easily.
- After blood loss, red cells rebuild at half speed, slower still short of iron, and rebuilding them spends iron.
- Wound infections climb faster.
- Moodle: Low Iron.

#### Gluten Intolerance (-4)

- Wheat (bread, pasta, baked goods, beer and dishes made with them) brings on a flare after a short onset.
- The flare ramps up: cramps, nausea, low mood. It takes about ten hours to clear; a second meal during one adds to it.
- Moodle: Gut Flare.

#### Smoker (-5, vanilla reworked)

- Cravings build at half vanilla's pace for a typical smoker, faster the heavier the habit. Withdrawal brings irritability (the vanilla Angry moodle, which now has [effects of its own](#anger)), hunger and restless sleep.
- A cigarette relieves only the craving it answers. To anyone else it is a buzz that fades as tolerance builds.
- Damaged lungs recover endurance slower and cough, worse on exertion and in the morning.
- Anyone who smokes regularly gains the trait; three weeks without tobacco loses it. After that, drink and stress bring cravings back, and a smoke is a coin flip to relapse.
- Smoking speeds up caffeine clearance, lets a wound infection climb faster and slows a deep wound's healing.
- Rare nicotine gum (new item) eases quitting.
- Moodle: Nicotine Craving. The sandbox option Nicotine Craving Moodle hides it, so the craving has to be read from irritability and stress.
- [Strain](#strain): withdrawal adds strain, and a cigarette eases only a quarter as much as it does for anyone else.

#### Vegetarian (-4)

- Meat, fish, insects and anything cooked with them are refused: the eat action will not start.
- Judged by food type, by name and by a dish's ingredients.

#### Germaphobe (-8)

- Dirty or bloody skin (the four worst parts count, not the average), dirty or bloody clothes, and the blood on the weapon in your hands (a dripping one reads 0.5 on its own) build stress and hold mood down.
- Getting clean is a real relief.
- Wounds are a fifth less likely to take an infection.
- Moodle: Filthy.

#### Sensory Issues (-8)

- Six senses feed one sensory load (0 to 100), every minute awake:
  - **Touch:** wool against the skin (jumpers, wooly hats, a balaclava, anything named wool or knit), denim a little, tight headgear (helmets, gas and face masks), damp, musty, never-washed or dryer-sheet clothes (laundry's itch, the one Sensitive Skin feels) and being wet through. Every garment's tooltip says how it feels: Soft (green: cotton, silk, satin, fleece, flannel, velvet, cashmere), or Scratchy, Rough (denim) or Tight (red); shoes and leather say nothing. Shown with laundry on or off.
  - **Grime:** blood and dirt on the skin and clothes, a bloody weapon in hand (Germaphobe's reading).
  - **Sound:** the loudest sound heard lately (gunshots, alarms, horns, banging; it fades by half a minute), ringing ears, a running engine, thunder. Deaf hears none of it, Hard of Hearing half, Keen Hearing more; ear muffs cut it by 60%, shooting ear protectors by 80%, and noise-cancelling headphones shut it out completely.
  - **Crowds:** zombies in sight (one or two hardly register, ten is the most) and any right on top of you. The heaviest sense.
  - **Light:** bright sun outdoors; sunglasses cut it.
  - **Smell:** corpses close by.
- The load climbs toward what the senses add up to and drains slowly while things are quiet (0.75 a minute at the least): faster indoors with nothing in sight, at home, in the dark, sitting down, in a bath (x3) or a shower (x2) with Take A Bath And Shower, and fastest asleep. A bath's soaking is not a touch trigger until that mod says you are dry. The input also adds stress every minute.
- **On Edge** (30): stress held at 25%. **Overwhelmed** (60): stress 50%, unhappiness 25, stamina back at 80%, and every timed action you start takes a fifth longer. **Overloaded** (90, until under 50): stress 75%, unhappiness 40, stamina at 60%, actions take 40% longer, and one of two: a **meltdown** (panic held at 40 and anger at half, with everything [anger](#anger) does; a one-in-ten chance a minute of throwing down what you hold, and a one-in-five chance of an outburst that zombies 20 tiles off hear) or a **shutdown** (stamina back at half, no sprinting, bored and low).
- **Worn thin:** each overload makes the next one come sooner until you sleep a night (four hours in one go): the triggers count 15% more per overload, up to three.
- **Food:** two safe and two disliked texture groups out of eight (crunchy, mushy, slimy, chewy, mixed dishes, strong-tasting, plain, sweet), told at the start and shown on food tooltips. At least one safe group keeps (crunchy, mushy, plain or sweet). A disliked group wins over a safe one, and a cooked dish of three or more things is mixed. Safe food takes 20 off the load and lifts the mood; disliked food stresses, upsets, adds load and may be gagged on halfway. Overloaded, only safe food goes down. A food in neither group, eaten calmly three whole times, becomes a safe food of its own.
- **Stims:** right-click a yo-yo, a puzzle cube, dice, a rubber band, a key ring, a toy (Fidget with it) or a plush or doll (Hold it close): 30 off the load and some stress, half that again within the hour. You can keep walking while you do it; running or aiming stops it.
- **Noise-cancelling headphones** (new item, for anyone): worn on the head, they make you deaf while worn: you hear nothing at all, zombies included, and Sensory Issues takes no noise. Take them off and your hearing comes straight back (a character who is deaf anyway stays deaf). Found in electronics and music stores, army electronics and therapists' desks, a fifth as often as ordinary headphones.
- An overload is a migraine trigger, the load makes MS spoons cost up to half as much again, and falling asleep overwhelmed scores the night 15% lower.
- Moodle: Sensory Load (On Edge, Overwhelmed, Meltdown, Shutdown). Console: `sensory`, `sensory load <n>`, `sensory foods`, `sensory reroll`.

#### Alcoholic (-6)

- Moodle: Alcohol Withdrawal (craving, the shakes, delirium).

## For everyone

These run for every character, trait or not. Most have a switch on the Vitality Project sandbox page.

### Vitality

Diet, exercise and sleep roll into one slow score.

- **Diet:** fresh, varied food lifts it; junk and neglect drag it. Variety counts over three days.
- **Exercise:** training (the fitness regularity the exercise menu builds) or a day of hard activity (running, fighting, chopping, heavy work, read from the body's metabolic rate). Activity alone keeps you at neutral; training takes you higher.
- **Sleep:** the night's score, below.
- **Fit and Thriving:** faster endurance recovery, mood and stress relief, faster healing, cold resistance, resistance to wound infection. Thriving alone adds a kilo of base carry weight and 1.5x Fitness and Strength experience.
- **Run Down and Sluggish:** the reverse, except that health is never drained; Run Down alone loses the kilo.
- The conditions above read it too, and so does how fast the body clears an infection, a concussion, an unstitched wound and a smoker's lungs.
- A night scores on hours slept (7 is full) and how rested you woke, less for waking in the night. A shorter sleep that cleared the tiredness you went to bed with counts as full hours.
- A bad night sets a same-day Slept Badly moodle. A new character starts neutral and cannot fall below it for the first day.
- Moodle: Vitality.

### Sleep and light

The light on your square while asleep sets how deep the sleep is.

- **Dark:** tiredness drains faster, the night scores better.
- **Lit:** tiredness drains slower, the night scores worse, and anything brighter than reading light can wake you (up to about half the hours fully lit). Close the curtains, turn off the lights.
- Exhaustion, drink and sleeping pills sleep through it.
- Light wakes some people more easily: Restless Sleeper, Night Owl, Cat's Eyes, anyone within six hours of a coffee, and during a depressive episode or a migraine. Restless Sleeper's two halves of a night, up to three hours apart, score as one.
- Desensitized characters have nightmares.
- A bad night makes a depressive episode likelier; sleeping with the light on makes a migraine likelier and stops sleep shortening an attack as much.

### Home

Where you sleep most nights becomes home, and home does you good. Vanilla gives you no reason to be at your base; this is one.

- Each night (not a nap, not in a car) is filed where you fell asleep: the building, or the spot in the open. Three of the last seven nights in one place make it home, with a notice. Sleep more of them somewhere new and home moves there (ties go to the newer place); nothing has to be claimed. A night away neither adds to it nor undoes it.
- **At home** (the building plus four tiles, or twenty tiles of a camp), awake: boredom (0.3 a minute), unhappiness (0.08) and stress (0.0004) ease, a night slept there scores up to +0.05, and books read up to a tenth faster. Half strength at three nights, full at seven.
- **Away** two days and you miss it: over the next day the Missing Home, then Homesick, moodle rises and an unhappiness floor of 15 comes up with it. Walking back in lifts it at once, with 15 unhappiness and a tenth of stress off, and a notice.
- Homebody doubles the good and misses home after a day, harder; Nomad has none of it. Sandbox option Home turns it all off.
- Moodle: Home (Settling In, At Home; Missing Home, Homesick).

### Strain

Fighting wears on you over days. Stress empties itself in an afternoon; strain is the memory of the week. It never makes you fight worse: it slows what the fighting teaches, so grinding stops paying and going home does. Design and tuning: `plans/strain.md`, `plans/strain_sim.py`.

- **Builds with** kills (0.1 each, more as the day goes on: kill k since you last slept costs 0.1 x (1 + k/100); from a car a third), minutes panicking (0.1), new wounds (scratch 1, laceration 2, deep wound 3), being knocked down (1.5), a bite (50), health under 25 (50, once a day), fever and sepsis, food sickness, pain, withdrawal for an Alcoholic or Smoker, and a hangover (2).
- **Eases with** calm hours (0.375 an hour awake, two hours after the last fight, anywhere), a night's sleep (4 in a bed, 2 rough, by the night's quality), comforts (a wash or bath, fresh clothes, a Good Meal 2-4 by tier, an hour of reading, Pumped: 2 each, the second the same day half, then nothing; x1.5 at home, x2 for a Homebody; four different ones in a day make the day's comforts x1.25), being drunk (2 an hour, up to 8 a day) and a smoke (0.75, up to 3). A fever halves what rest and sleep take off.
- Rough numbers: a supply run of 25 kills is +5, a busy day of 75 is +17, a horde day of 150 is +41; a full day off at home is about -30, a lazy one or a calm day out about -10. Two horde days in a row are fine; the third breaks you.
- **Tiers** (moodle: Strain): At Ease (under 10: combat experience x1.1), Frayed (25), Worn Down (50: unhappiness held at 15), Burnt Out (75: at 30), Breaking Point (100).
- **Experience:** combat skills (weapons, Aiming, Reloading, Strength, Fitness) x1 at 25, x0.75 at 50, x0.4 at 75, x0.15 at 100. Other skills take half that penalty, only over 50.
- **Breaking Point:** strain holds at 100 until the first safe moment (ten minutes without a fight, no zombie within 12 tiles); then you sit down where you are for about half a minute and can do nothing else. Walking away or a zombie within 4 tiles stops it, and it comes again. Finished, strain drops to 60.
- The first week counts half (both sandbox options). Desensitized takes 0.6 of combat strain. The health panel shows the tier and whether it is easing.
- Sandbox: Strain, Strain Gained %, Strain Eased %, Lowest Combat Experience % (15), Strain Grace Days (7), Strain During Grace % (50), Breaking Point.
- **What else it touches:** Major Depressive Disorder episodes, Hallucinations, migraine attacks, MS flares and seizures all come more often the higher strain is (x1.33 Frayed, x2 Burnt Out). Burnt Out brings an ex-smoker's cravings back and holds Vitality under Thriving; At Ease lifts it a little. A sensory meltdown or shutdown adds 5. Panic counts only with a zombie within 15 tiles.
- **Trait links:** Endorphin Junkie's Pumped eases twice as much; Homemaker's fresh clothes twice as much, and Line-Dried is a comfort of its own; Keen Cook's meals count a tier up; in a depressive episode every comfort but a meal or a workout does half; a Nomad gets the home bonus in any bed slept in twice of the last seven nights.
- Console: `strain`, `strain set <n>`, `strain kill [n]`, `strain comfort <kind>`.

### Blood

Bleeding drains blood instead of health.

- **How fast:** by how bad the bleed is (the game's own bleeding clock), where it is (neck three times, head, thigh and groin half as much again, chest and belly a little more, hands and feet less), and the dressing: a bandage slows it to a tenth, a shard or bullet left in bleeds through the bandage, stitches stop it, [clotting powder](#wound-care) cuts it to a quarter.
- **15% lost, Pale:** endurance recovers slower.
- **30%, Light-headed:** no sprinting, anxious.
- **40%, Shock:** endurance capped, health draining, and you can pass out for 5 to 15 game minutes.
- **45%, Bleeding Out:** passing out more often. **Half your blood is death.**
- **Recovery:** volume comes back in about a day if you drink, and makes you thirsty. Red cells take about a week, faster fed, asleep and with good Vitality; while short you tire sooner.
- The wound itself still hurts until it is dressed and stitched, as in vanilla.
- Moodle: Blood Loss. The sandbox option Blood Loss turns it off (vanilla bleeding).

### Wound infection

Replaces vanilla's one-roll infection.

- **Each hour** a wound may take an infection: likelier for a deep wound or bite than a scratch, much more with a shard or bullet left in, open or under a spent bandage, or under dirty or bloody clothes. Far less once disinfected, and not at all while disinfectant or garlic is on it (or under a fresh alcohol bandage).
- Prone to Illness and Resilient count, and so do Diabetes (high sugar), Vitality, iron and smoking.
- **Incubation:** 8 to 16 hours unseen. Cleaning the wound then ends it.
- **Growth:** the wound's infection level (the health panel, the pain) climbs about 2 a day, and the wound stops healing. Disinfectant and garlic still push it back.
- **Level 5, spread:** disinfectant and garlic stop working and only antibiotics do. The fever (temperature, the Sick moodle, tiredness, thirst) follows hours to a day or two later. Past that, sepsis drains health, faster the worse it gets. Untreated it kills in a couple of days.
- **Antibiotics** are the game's own pills, one a dose. Each tops up a level in the blood that halves every six hours; while it is high enough the infection falls back. A dose every eight hours keeps it there; ten doses finish a course (a box holds 12). Stop early once it is gone and it has a coin flip to come back. Vanilla's one-pill cure is gone.
- A fever makes for a bad night, brings on migraines, raises blood sugar and irritates asthma. Sepsis brings delirium.
- Zombie infection is untouched, except for [Resilient](#resilient-10-vanilla-reworked)'s chance to beat it.
- Moodle: Infection. A sandbox option turns it off.

### Wound care

- **Bandages** wear out over about a day, faster wet and faster still as blood soaks through. Spent, a bandage only halves a bleed and invites infection, and you are told to change it.
- **Unstitched deep wounds** heal at about a third of the speed, and hard use of the limb can open them again.
- **Fresh stitches** can tear: swinging a weapon with a stitched arm, sprinting or running on a stitched leg, less on the torso, less under a bandage. They hold better as they heal and are sound once the game's stitch time passes 40. Torn, the wound is open and bleeding again.
- **Stitching is a First Aid roll:** 45% rough at level 0, 5% less a level, never under 3%; a suture needle or needle holder takes it to 0.6 of that, Steady Hands halves it. Rough stitches knit at half speed, tear twice as easily, ache, and let infection in like an open wound until they are sound. A stitcher at level 3 or more can tell. Take them out and stitch again.
- **Splinting is a First Aid roll:** half the time badly set at level 0, 6% less a level, never under 2%. Set badly, the bone heals at half speed and hurts; only a setter at level 3 or more can tell. Take it off and set it again.
- Moving on a broken leg with no splint makes the break worse.
- A sandbox option turns it off.
- **Clotting powder** (new item, 5 uses; medicine cabinets, first aid kits, doctors' and medics' bags, pet shops, salons, army medical): right-click a bleeding part in the health panel with the dressing off. A scratch or cut stops bleeding; a deep wound's bleed is halved and then cut to a quarter for 12 hours (it still needs stitches), and a bandage over it soaks that much slower. It stings, less with First Aid. Barely helps with a shard or bullet still in (x0.6); a hemophiliac's bleed only halves and never stops. The clot breaks if the wound tears or opens again. Fear of Blood can faint at it, like a bandage. Not switched off with wound care.

### What First Aid tells you

The health panel's wound list is written for the examiner's First Aid level: your own, or the doctor's for a patient, read live.

- **0 to 2:** what anyone can see. Scratched, cut, a bad cut, bitten, bleeding, something stuck in it, a dressing, "might be broken", and an infection only once it is red and swollen. No severities.
- **3 to 5:** the game's own detail, plus infected, a dressing wearing thin, a badly set bone and rough stitches.
- **6 to 8:** how far an infection has got and how stitches are holding.
- **9 and 10:** an infection that has not shown yet, and how far a break has healed.
- Wraps whichever list drawing is installed, so it works over mods that replace it (NestedHealthInfo), and leaves the debug view alone.

### Concussion

- **Causes:** a hard landing (from about the second floor up), a car crash (by the speed the car loses: nothing under about 25 km/h lost, so running down a zombie at speed is nothing; a wall at 50 a good chance; certain and severe from 70), being hit by a car, or a weapon hit that takes health off the head. A helmet makes it less likely and less bad.
- **Dazed:** a headache, drowsy.
- **Concussed:** sick to the stomach too, bright daylight makes the headache worse, slower to get your breath back, and running or fighting can bring on a dizzy fall; a quarter of the time it knocks you out briefly.
- **Badly concussed:** knocked out on the spot for 5 to 15 game minutes.
- **Healing:** rest heals it, sleep twice as fast; running and fighting stop it healing. A second knock lands on top of the first.
- **The headache after:** a chance (half, plus half the severity) of a post-concussion headache one to two and a half days later, often after the concussion has cleared. It builds over six hours to a pain worse than the knock's own, holds, and fades over its last twelve hours, lasting a day to two by severity. Sleep gets through it half as fast again; daylight makes it worse; painkillers dull it.
- It brings on migraine attacks and makes light wake you more easily. Shown on the head in the health panel.
- Moodle: Concussion. A sandbox option turns it off.

### Passing out

Shock, a concussion and Fear of Blood can all put you on the floor.

- You fall, end up sitting, the screen goes black, and you can do nothing until you come round, for game minutes but never under eight real seconds. After those eight seconds time runs fast, as it does asleep, and goes back to normal when you come round (a paused game stays paused). You come round sitting on the floor and get up yourself. Zombies can still get to you.
- In a vehicle there is no fall: you slump in the seat, and a driver's engine cuts out so the car rolls to a stop.
- A faint is shallow: anything wounding you jolts you awake. A concussion knockout is not.

### Sunburn

- Outdoors in sunshine (day, no rain, less under cloud, nothing at 5 C and full from 20 C, full from 11 to 3 and gone by 7 morning and evening), every body part no clothing covers builds exposure. About three hours of midday summer sun burns bare skin; "your skin feels hot" comes first.
- A burnt part hurts, on that part, for a day, easing over the last six hours. The game adds the parts up: one burnt hand is a nuisance, a whole body burnt is agony for most of the day.
- It makes for a worse night, and the health panel shows Sunburnt on the part. Shade or indoors lets exposure fade.
- Skin toughens: each burn makes the next take longer, and the tan fades over a month. Outdoorsman skin takes twice as long to burn.
- **Sun block** (new item, as common as toothpaste; 8 coats) keeps the sun off all bare skin for 8 hours a coat. You are told when it wears off.
- Moodle: Sunburn, with a good side while sun block is on. A sandbox option turns it off.

### Dehydration

- Vanilla thirst only costs health at the top end. Hours at Thirsty or worse (twice as fast Parched, three times Dying of Thirst) now build a load: a headache, tiredness and slower endurance recovery.
- It drains in about two hours once you have drunk.
- Moodle: Dehydration. A sandbox option turns it off.

### Anger

The vanilla Angry moodle does nothing in the base game. Here it costs you, from Annoyed up; Irritated is only the warning. A smoker's withdrawal raises it (up to Angry at full withdrawal), and so does an Alcoholic's (up to Annoyed while craving and Angry once the shakes start, at the deepest habit; a drink or sleep takes it back off). Anger from another mod counts the same.

- **Rough:** every melee hit has an extra chance to wear the weapon: half as much again at Annoyed, double at Angry, two and a half times at Furious. Anger never takes a weapon's last point. Furious swings cost extra endurance.
- **Loud:** from Angry up the character curses out loud now and then (about once an hour, more often and louder at Furious), and zombies nearby hear it. Never asleep, and never within three minutes of a cough.
- **Can't concentrate:** reading takes longer: x1.15 Annoyed, x1.3 Angry, x1.5 Furious.
- **Sloppy fine work:** splints set badly and stitches come out rough more often (x1.25, x1.5, x2), and installing or removing a vehicle part is 5, 10 or 20 points less likely to succeed and as much more likely to damage the part. The mechanics window shows the angry chance.
- The moodle's descriptions say so. The sandbox option Anger Has Effects turns it off.

### Hangovers

- Drink past a light buzz and a hangover waits for you to sober up, or to wake: at least six hours of headache, low mood, thirst and tiredness, longer and with nausea after a heavy night.
- Two hours properly drunk is a full-strength one, and even a small one is felt (never under two fifths strength).
- Endurance comes back slower while it lasts (up to 40% slower), and bright daylight outdoors makes the headache worse.
- Painkillers only dull it: one taken while hungover works for half as long.
- A drink hides it and stops the clock, and counts toward the next one.
- Alcoholics carry the habit as tolerance: more drink to feel it, withdrawal sooner and harder, hangovers a little milder.
- Moodle: Hangover. A sandbox option turns it off.

### Drink relief

- Vanilla treats any sip of alcohol as a full dose of beta blockers and painkillers, however small. That is undone.
- Relief follows the Drunk moodle instead: pain reduction of 20, 40, 60 or 80 by level, and panic that settles at a quarter, half, three quarters or the full beta-blocker rate.
- Being drunk lifts the mood too: unhappiness, stress and boredom drain while you are awake, faster the drunker you are. Tipsy takes the edge off (3 unhappiness an hour); blind drunk takes 30 an hour, so an evening of it clears even a severe mood. The hangover is the price. A depressive episode keeps its own floor.
- A sandbox option turns it off.

### Medication

One list of drugs that every trait reads (`DanTraits_Meds.lua`).

- **Daily drugs** (beta blockers, anticonvulsants, metformin, baclofen, amantadine) have a level in your system that every pill tops up and a half-life drains, and a build-up: a little after the first pill, fully after a few days of regular doses. A missed dose lets them fade slowly, not all at once.
- **Rescue drugs** (diazepam, sumatriptan, prednisone) work at once while they are in your system.
- **Everything else** that is taken is on the list too (inhaler, nicotine gum, painkillers, sleeping tablets, caffeine pills, the insulin pen's doses): each keeps what it does and gains a level, a side-effect roll and a too-many effect. Four painkillers at once bring strong nausea, too many sleeping tablets a blackout risk, five inhaler puffs or four caffeine pills the shakes (and chest pain twice as likely for Heart Condition).
- **Side effects:** on each day a drug is taken, a small chance (3%, sandbox option Medication Side Effect Chance) of its mild side effect for a few hours. Stomach ones bring on the game's nausea moodle. Some act on anyone the whole time: prednisone's hunger, baclofen's drowsiness, amantadine's dry mouth.
- **Wearing off:** a drug's moodle (beta blockers, anticonvulsants) is paler green while it builds up and full green once it has, and goes out when the drug leaves your system. You are told when a protecting drug lapses.
- **Medication reminder:** a character on a daily drug for one of their traits (beta blockers, anticonvulsants, metformin, baclofen, amantadine, antidepressants) gets a Medication moodle: **Medication Due** from two hours before the next dose (once-a-day drugs every 24 hours, the others every 12), **Missed Medication** once it has worn off and the effect is slipping. Gone once the effect has faded completely. Sandbox option Medication Reminder Moodle turns it off.
- **Health panel:** under the overall body status, a Daily Medication section lists those drugs with when each was last taken and when the next is due (green), due now (orange) or worn off (red). Your own character only.
- **Starting Medication** (sandbox option, on): a character who starts with a condition starts on its drug, built up, with the medication. Off, they find their own.
- **Diazepam** (new item): the panic drop vanilla beta blockers used to give, for about an hour and a half. Beta blockers now only do what Heart Condition needs.
- **Baclofen as a muscle relaxant** (for anyone; cyclobenzaprine was folded into it, and an old bottle of it still works as baclofen): one tablet works for about 12 hours: stiffness fades twice as fast, a pulled muscle's pain and stiffness halve (it heals no sooner), a Bad Back's pain and stiffness halve (a third off thrown out). Mild drowsiness, far drowsier with a drink. Without MS, training on it gives 15% less experience and a pull is half as likely again; with MS it is the daily pill and training is as usual. Too many at once: limp and barely awake.
- **Adrenaline auto-injector** (new item, rare: likeliest in an ambulance or a doctor's bag, now and then in a bathroom cabinet or a handbag; one shot): for an emergency, anyone. For two hours no tiredness at all, full stamina that comes back half as fast again, but stress, shaking hands (a swing can drop the weapon) and, with a Heart Condition, strain three times as fast. Then the crash: the tiredness it held off comes back over half an hour with a quarter on top (and a little more), then six hours of comedown (stamina back at half speed, low mood, a headache, the shakes for the first hour); asleep it passes four times as fast. A 1 in 4 chance of a heart attack, sometime in the first 40 minutes, and each shot within a day adds another 1 in 4 (the fourth is certain). A shot during the crash only puts it off and makes the next one worse. Beta blockers blunt it: half the lift, half the risk. Moodle: Adrenaline.
- **Pill Caddy** (new item, uncommon): a belt-worn weekly organiser that holds medication only. Capacity 1 (Organized makes it 2). Found in medicine cabinets, bedside drawers, handbags, on pharmacy shelves and on the odd zombie, and a found one often has a bottle or two in it. A new character with a medical trait has a 1% chance to start with one.
- Daily drug bottles hold 30 pills, most of the mod's bottles spawn partly used (Prednisone, Baclofen and Amantadine full), and part bottles merge like vanilla's. Every tooltip says what the drug treats, how to take it and its side effects. Antidepressants keep Depression's own three-day regimen, with a moodle of their own: paler green while a pill's coverage runs, full green once the three days are up, gone when a day is missed.

### Laundry

Washing and drying clothes take some care (`DanTraits_Laundry*.lua`; design in [plans/laundry.md](plans/laundry.md)). It has its own sandbox page, Vitality: Laundry, and one switch turns it all off. Moved here from Dan's Vanilla Fixes, which no longer touches washing.

- **Hand washing has floors.** Without soap, blood and dirt only come down to 40% per patch; with soap or cleaning liquid, to 20%. Carry a **washboard** (new item: laundry rooms and cleaning shelves, or made from a plank with sheet metal and nails, or carved with Carving 2) and it is 25% and 10%, at half as long again. Only a washing machine gets clothes fully clean (and washes them fresh), whether or not you stay beside it while it runs. A garment with nothing to gain is not washed, and the character says why. Take A Bath And Shower's bath wash keeps the same floors.
- **Wringing leaves clothes damp** (50%, shoes 70%), so it saves time but not the drying.
- **Damp clothes go musty.** A garment that stays 30% wet or more for 6 hours turns musty; worn, the hours count half as fast again. Worn clothes dry at a quarter of the game's own pace (sandbox Drying on the Body; the game dries a soaked shirt on you in about 20 minutes), and every hour worn damp adds odour, so clothes straight out of the washer are best dried first. Musty clothes stay musty until washed. Ways to dry: a dryer, body heat, any container (the game's own, 20 points an hour), **by the fire** (right-click a lit campfire, fireplace, barbecue or stove, or a damp garment near one), or a **clothesline**.
- **Sweat makes clothes stale.** Worn clothes build up odour: slowly just worn, faster with hard work and heat. Underwear, shirts, trousers and socks go fastest, jackets and hats at half the rate, jewellery and bags not at all. A soapless hand wash takes odour down to 30, anything else all of it. Clean but stale clothes can be washed from a water source's menu: Freshen up clothes.
- **Fresh Clothes.** When everything you wear (shoes aside) is clean, dry and fresh, the good side of the moodle shows: unhappiness eases half a point an hour and stress a little. **Line-Dried** (half or more of it dried on a clothesline in daylight since its last wash) doubles that. Germaphobe gets twice the benefit.
- **Moodle: Stale Clothes, then Musty Clothes**, from the worst garment worn. Each lifts unhappiness a little, stopping at 20 (30 for musty); Germaphobe feels it twice as much, and stale clothes count as dirty for Germaphobe's clothing share. A musty garment over a wound counts as dirty clothing for infection.
- **Blood sets in.** Blood left 3 days on a garment leaves a stain (half of it, at most 20%) that no wash gets under, washing machine included. **Bleach** gets it out: Bleach stains out at a water source, 0.1 L a garment. Hard on fabric.
- **Washing wears fabric.** Each wash has a small chance to take a condition point off: 1% by hand, 3% on a washboard, 4% in a machine, 25% with bleach. Never a hole, never under 1.
- **Clotheslines.** The pulley clotheslines in yards each hold 15, and you can put one up yourself: right-click the ground with two long sticks, planks or saplings and two lengths of rope, twine, sheet strips, denim strips or wire carried (Put up a clothesline, east-west or north-south); one you put up can be taken down again. Clothes on a line dry with the weather: fast in sun, wind and warmth, slowly at night or in the cold, and rain soaks them again; under a roof, at a steady pace. Daylight also airs out a little staleness (never under 30).
- **Keeping clothes clean.** A worn **poncho** takes the blood and dirt for the parts it covers. The garbage bag poncho wears as it is used (a chance at a condition point each time blood or dirt lands on it, and each ten minutes of hard work or heat) and falls apart at nothing; besides the game's recipe there is a rough one (one bag and a knife or scissors, no skill). **Fabric protector** (new item, with cleaning supplies and in shoe shops) and **fabric wax** (made from two candles and an empty tin) treat a garment so only half the blood and dirt that lands on it stays, for about three washes.
- **Dryer sheets.** A box of 20 (new item: laundry rooms and cleaning shelves). Right-click a dryer with the box on you to put one in with the load: everything that comes out dry goes stale half as fast once worn, until its next wash. One sheet a load; it waits in the dryer until a run, and the option is greyed while the dryer runs or has a sheet in. Clothes put away never go stale, sheet or not.
- Garment tooltips show the state: damp and how long before musty, stale or musty, a set-in stain, fabric protector left, dryer sheet, washes so far.
- **Load the washer in one click.** Right-click a washing machine (or a combo washer/dryer set to wash, or a stacked pair's washer): Put dirty clothes in the washer (N) walks over and moves every dirty or bloody garment you carry, bags included, into it. What you wear stays on. This one works with laundry off too.
- **Traits.** Sweaty (-2) and Cool Customer (+3) change how fast clothes go stale, Slob (+2) is not bothered either way, Homemaker (+4) washes better, and Sensitive Skin (-2) itches in damp, musty, unwashed or dryer-sheet clothes. Laundry reaches into other traits too: Nomad minds stale clothes half as much, a Homebody's clothes line-dried at home lift twice as much, bleaching indoors irritates Asthma, washing bloody clothes stresses Fear of Blood, and every garment scrubbed on a washboard strains a Bad Back. With laundry off, none of it does anything.

### Reading

Reading for pleasure is something to carry on a run (`DanTraits_Reading*.lua`; design and as-built notes in [plans/reading.md](plans/reading.md)). Its own sandbox page, Vitality: Reading, with one switch to turn it off. Skill books, recipe magazines, fliers and brochures stay vanilla. Built in Dan's Vanilla Fixes, moved here on 2026-10-15.

- **Every title is tagged.** Every vanilla novel, comic, magazine and newspaper has one of 18 genres, a page count and a hidden quality of one to five stars, from a table generated out of the game (`DanTraits/tools/gen_reading_data.py`). Books from other mods read as Popular Fiction, 120 pages, three stars.
- **Long reads.** A novel is 1.5 minutes a page (half a minute for picture media), scaled by the game's Minutes Per Page and by everything that changes vanilla's read time (Fast and Slow Reader, glasses, sitting). A 350-page novel is about nine hours, six with Fast Reader. The relief arrives page by page, not as a lump at the end, and the stress and unhappiness part of it climbs with the sitting: 0.4 of the rate on the first page, 1.3 after an hour without stopping (20 minutes for picture media), so an afternoon lost in a book does more than the same pages in snatches. Put it down and pick the same title up within 15 minutes and you are still in it.
- **Bookmarks.** Your place is kept per character and on the copy. Right-click **Read a chapter (~30 min)** reads a chapter's worth and stops.
- **Taste.** Each character likes one genre and dislikes another, found out about 30 pages in. A liked book does a quarter more, a disliked one half as much and brings a little boredom back.
- **Good Read moodle** on finishing a book: Good Read, Great Read (four or five stars, or a liked genre) or Loved It (both). About two days for a novel, half a day for a comic or magazine, with one bonus set by the genre that the moodle names: a stat that eases (panic, stress, low moods, boredom, stamina), a few skills' XP at +5%, or Serious Reading's faster pages. One at a time; a better finish takes over.
- **Real books.** About 200 real titles from before July 1993; a novel in loot has an 8% chance (sandbox) of being one from its genre.
- **Vitality's say.** At home a page does 15% more good by the home's strength (30% for a Homebody); concussed, half as much and a quarter slower; a Really Bad Day's first day gets a quarter more comfort from any book; in the 40s without reading glasses a page takes a tenth longer, in the 50s 15%.
- **This Is Your Life:** finished books and tastes found out go in its survivor journal.
- The tooltip shows the genre, how you feel about it once you know, pages read of the total, and your verdict once finished. With Dan's Vanilla Fixes on, its item info adds the time left to finish.

### Fitness

Training with a body that tires, recovers and rewards a routine (`DanTraits_Fitness*.lua`; design and as-built notes in [plans/fitness-overhaul.md](plans/fitness-overhaul.md)). Sandbox option Fitness Training turns it off; Pulled Muscles turns off just the strains; Training Experience (%) sets how fast Fitness and Strength grow (100 by default).

- **Sets in reps.** The fitness window asks for reps, not minutes (10 to 60; stretching still takes minutes). The set ends on its last rep or when you are exhausted (about 50 reps, 38 of the heavy ones), with a bar over the head counting them off.
- **Four muscle groups**: arms, chest, core, legs. Each rep loads the groups the exercise works, the same for a slow squat as a quick push-up: a set of 50 leaves a group at 0.83 of a full load. Jogging, carrying and hard work load them by the minute. Load wears off over about a day and a half awake, twice as fast asleep, faster with good Vitality and young, slower old.
- **Train it at the right time.** A group recovered and trained 20 to 96 hours ago is Ready: +25% experience. Come in Sore and it is -40%, Overworked -70%; past 45 minutes on one group in a session, half. The fitness window shows each group's state and the experience the exercise would give now.
- **Pulled muscle.** Training sore muscles, or one group for too long, can pull a muscle: pain, a stiff part, and that group cannot be trained for a day and a half. Improvised weights make it likelier, a weight bench less.
- **Pumped** after 15 minutes of training: three hours of stress, low mood and boredom easing, and it counts as exercise against depression. **Overtrained** after seven hard days in a row: less experience, worse nights, slower stamina, until two easy days. A hard day is 40 minutes of training plus 6 for each level of Fitness and Strength (100 minutes at 5 / 5, 160 at 10 / 10).
- **New exercises.** Curls, One-Arm Curls and Overhead Press with whatever heavy thing you carry, named for it (Log Curls, Car Battery One-Arm Curls): a log, a propane tank, a full bucket, a sandbag, a car battery, a paint can. The heavier, the more it builds. **Stretching**: no experience, twice the recovery while you do it, a progress bar over the head, and ten minutes leaves you Loosened Up (faster recovery, stiffness fades sooner, Arthritis and MS eased 10%). **Jogging**, a **loaded carry** and **hard work** count by themselves.
- **Weight bench**: within three tiles, barbell and dumbbell work is worth 15% more and strains less. Right-click a weight bench or treadmill: Work Out.
- **Training plans.** Pick one in the fitness window: Full Body for everyone; Upper/Lower, Push Pull Legs, First Five Miles, Strongman and Active Recovery from magazines (a Fitness Instructor knows them all). Do a day's job and you are On Plan: +15% experience and recovery, and a better Vitality exercise score. Miss a job and you are off it for the two days after, then back (Regimented forgives one miss a week). The window shows today's job, whether you are on plan (or why not, and when it comes back) and the last seven days, with a notice when you go on or off.
- **Magazines.** Five, in gym lockers, sports shelves and magazine racks. Each teaches its plan and adds to all experience for good: Strength +10% (the two Iron Monthly), Fitness +10% (Pavement, Limber), both +5% (Strongman Quarterly).
- **Traits:** Well Conditioned, Weightlifter, Natural Runner, Regimented, Endorphin Junkie, Lingering Soreness, Injury Prone, Exercise Averse, Inflexible (see the trait list).
- **Your other traits:** a workout lowers blood sugar with either type of Diabetes, during it and for hours after; burpees set off Asthma like a run; heavy lifting and burpees strain a Heart Condition; 20 minutes on the legs in a day helps Brittle bones; improvised two-handed lifts load a Bad Back, and a Bad Back pulls more easily in the core.
- **Hot water bottle** (the game's own, found empty in bedside tables, closets, wardrobes and kitchen drawers): fill it with water and heat it on a stove, in a microwave or by a fire. It stays Hot for an hour and Warm to three, cold at four (in bed hot 1.5 hours and cold at six; outdoors below freezing hot half an hour and cold at two); its tooltip says how long. Right-click it, Hold on sore muscles, and pick a group: for up to 30 minutes that group recovers twice as fast Hot (1.5x Warm), its stiffness fades, a pulled muscle there heals about two hours sooner, a Bad Back eases on the core, and Arthritis's joints ache less while you hold it and for an hour after. Carried Hot or Warm while asleep, every group recovers a quarter faster (15% Warm) and a cold night (10C or below) scores a little better. Emptying it loses the heat.
- Moodles: Sore Muscles (Sore, Overworked, Pulled Muscle), Pumped (Loosened Up, Pumped; Restless for an Endorphin Junkie), Overtrained.

### Cooking

A proper cooked meal does more than fill you (`DanTraits_Cooking.lua`; design and the vanilla facts in [plans/cooking-overhaul.md](plans/cooking-overhaul.md)). Sandbox option Cooking turns it off; the training buffs need [fitness](#fitness) on as well.

- **A proper meal** is 300 calories of one dish (bites within an hour add up). **Home-cooked**: two ingredients or more (a sandwich or salad stops here). **Hearty**: cooked, three ingredients, the cook at Cooking 3. **Chef's**: cooked, four ingredients or three and a spice, Cooking 7. Burnt, rotten or still raw: nothing; stale: a tier down. The cook's level goes on the dish as they add ingredients, so a good cook feeds everyone well. The tooltip shows the tier for you.
- **Good Meal.** Low mood and stress ease for 3, 4 or 5 hours by tier, a base plus a quarter, half or three quarters of what the dish itself lifted (spices and good ingredients count). A quarter more at home, half again for a Homebody; the same dish twice in a day does half. A dish with a texture Sensory Issues can't stand gives none. It counts as comfort food against depression while it glows.
- **Fuelled** for four hours: Fitness and Strength experience +5, 10 or 15%. And one more by what the dish is mostly: **protein**, muscles recover 10 / 20 / 30% faster for six hours; **carbs**, 5 / 10 / 15% of the stamina you spend comes back for three; **fat**, hunger rises 5 / 10 / 15% slower for four. Vanilla already gives +50% Strength experience while your protein is high, so protein here is recovery only.
- **Side stitch**: training or running while Stuffed or Full to Bursting can stiffen your side.
- **Diet goals** in the fitness window: **Bulk**, Strength +10% while you are putting on weight; **Cut**, Fitness +10% while you are losing it, and the weight comes off a quarter faster. Fuelled with a hard day's training in, weight drifts back toward 78 to 82 kg.
- All of fitness's experience boosts together top out at double (before the Training Experience option).
- Moodle: Good Meal (Good Meal, Great Meal; Side Stitch on the bad side).

## Base Unloader

A separate mod in this repo (`BaseUnloader/`, its own Workshop item once uploaded) that puts a loot run away for you. Design talked through on 2026-10-16; built the same day, not yet play-tested.

- Right-click the car (**Unload trunk to base**), any container, or items in a loot window (**Unload to base**), or your own items (**Put away what I am carrying**). A window opens: left, what is in the source grouped by type with where each type goes; right, every container in the base with its name, kind, room, floor, distance from you and how full it is, zone by zone, with each zone's tagged containers (blue stripe, under "Tagged") apart from the rest (under "Not tagged"). The base is the base zones you draw (**Base zones...** on the world right-click menu, or **Zones** in the window) within Zone Reach (40 tiles) of the source. Drawing uses the game's click-and-drag zone tool, but the zones are Base Unloader's own, never animal zones. Pieces with the same name are one zone, each side up to 60 tiles, and a zone takes in Floors Searched (3) above and below its floor. With no zone drawn nearby, the base is the building you are in or next to plus a margin, together with a circle round the source (sandbox: Building Margin 4, Nearby Radius 12).
- Drag a type onto a container to say that type lives there; the rule is remembered for the save, so the next run needs almost no dragging. Items dragged straight out of the vanilla inventory windows onto a container row do the same. Ctrl while dropping sends just those items, this run only. **Suggest** fills the gaps from what the base already holds (the container with most of that type, then the fridge or freezer for perishables, then the category, then a container of a suitable kind). Right-click a type to unassign it or its whole category; right-click a container to send everything unassigned there or clear its assignments.
- Hover a container row and it lights up in the world. Double-click it (or right-click, in the world too) to rename it; the loot window shows the name as well, since it is the game's own container name with a copy kept in the save.
- **Base inventory...** (world right-click menu) lists everything stored in all your base zones, containers, bags and floor, by type and container: search, sort by any column, hover to light the container up, double-click to walk there. Zones out of range keep their last count, shown grey with its age. Right-click items of one type and their menu says how many are at base ("At base: 12 (House 8, Shed 4)"); click that for the inventory searched for them. Containers named with Smarter Storage show that name. Ideas from Home Inventory Improved.
- **Start**: the character walks to the source, loads a trip's worth into worn bags (then hands) within the carry weight, walks round the base nearest-container first putting things down, and goes back for more until the source holds nothing with a home. Favourites and anything worn or held are never touched. A closed trunk lid is opened on the way. Each step is planned from what is actually in the source and the bags, so a zombie, a key press or a reload only pauses it: **Resume unloading** is on every right-click menu while a plan is saved, **Forget the unfinished run** drops it. If two steps in a row move nothing (a container that refuses the item) the run stops rather than loop.
- Files: `shared/BaseUnloader_Core.lua` (keys, saved data in `ModData.getOrCreate("BaseUnloader")`, rules, grouping, suggestions, trip planning: pure, tested), `client/BaseUnloader_Scan.lua` (zone, container rows, names, places, item records, sources), `client/BaseUnloader_Run.lua` (the step machine over `luautils.walkToContainer` and `ISInventoryTransferAction`, one destination per step because walkToContainer clears the queue, a marker action ending each step), `client/BaseUnloader_UI.lua` (the window), `client/BaseUnloader_Zones.lua` (drawing base zones and the zone window), `client/BaseUnloader_Stock.lua` (the base inventory window), `client/BaseUnloader_Menu.lua` (the menus; the vehicle menu is a wrapped `ISVehicleMenu.FillMenuOutsideVehicle`). Deploy with `python deploy.py --unloader`; test with `python tests/run_tests.py baseunloader`. Singleplayer only for now (the saved data is not transmitted).

```

DanTraits/common/        empty; Build 42 expects it beside 42/
  media/lua/shared/      one file per trait + DanTraits.lua (core helpers, eat/pill hooks)
  media/lua/client/      context menus, moodles, telemetry for the companion app
  media/scripts/         trait and item definitions
DanTraits/tools/         make_icons.py (draws the moodle, item and age trait icons), make_reading_icon.py
                         (Good Read), gen_reading_data.py (the reading table, from the game jar)
tests/                   offline tests (fengari); python tests/run_tests.py
DansVanillaFixes/        Dan's Vanilla Fixes, a separate mod (python deploy.py --patch)
BaseUnloader/            Base Unloader, a separate mod (python deploy.py --unloader)
workshop/                workshop.txt and preview.png for the Steam Workshop page
deploy.py                copy the mod to ~/Zomboid/mods (or --pull edits back, or --workshop)
```

Mod data is one table per player (`getModData().DanTraits`) with a prefix per system (`alc*`, `nic*`, `inf*`, ...); the companion app's Live tab groups it by that prefix. A renamed key is moved by `DanTraits_MigrateModData` in the core.

## Workflow

1. Edit in this repo.
2. `python tests/run_tests.py`
3. `python deploy.py` then restart the game (traits, items and Lua are read at boot). While the Workshop upload folder (`~/Zomboid/Workshop/DanTraits`) exists the game loads that copy instead of `~/Zomboid/mods/DanTraits`, so the deploy mirrors into both and they never differ.
4. The companion app (the `pz-companion` repo beside this one, `Companion.bat`) for a live readout at http://127.0.0.1:8642 with buttons to trigger any trait's events, the console log, and later Chronicle and music. The game writes the telemetry while the companion is running (it leaves a heartbeat file in `~/Zomboid/Lua`) or in debug mode (`-debug`); the command channel only works in debug mode, and nothing runs in multiplayer. Design: `plans/companion.md`.

To publish: `python deploy.py --workshop` builds `~/Zomboid/Workshop/DanTraits` (workshop.txt, preview.png and `Contents/mods/DanTraits` without the dev tools), then upload it from the game's main menu, Workshop. After the first upload the game writes the item's `id=` into that workshop.txt; the build keeps it, so later uploads update the same item.

The game's Lua (Kahlua) allows 200 locals and 60 upvalues per function, the file's top level included, so new traits go in their own file (`require "DanTraits"` for the shared helpers). It also lacks `next`, `assert`, `xpcall`, `string.gmatch` and `string.rep`. The offline tests run on standard Lua and would not notice any of that, so `tests/lint_kahlua.py` (run first by `run_tests.py`) checks for it, and checks the translation files too: valid JSON, no duplicate keys, and no bare `%` (the game formats UI strings; write `%%` for a percent sign, `%1` for a placeholder). Calling a Java method an object doesn't have fails quietly inside `pcall` but dumps a stack trace to the log every time, and the tests' stand-ins can't notice, so `tests/check_api.py` (also run by `run_tests.py`) checks the method names called on body parts, body damage, players, stats and weapons against the installed game's jar.

## Status and known issues

Version 1.4.0, Build 42, singleplayer only (multiplayer is untested and not supported). What changed in each update is in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); the Workshop page carries the short version.

- **A Really Bad Day** is not balanced yet. Its glass shard killed even a character with no other traits, so for now the wound is an infected cut and the trait gives 8 points instead of 12, until a proper balance is found.
- **Invisible character** (seen once, in debug mode, after a heart-attack blackout and some console commands): the model vanished, reloading didn't fix it, and restarting the game did. Nothing in the mod touches visibility; the likeliest cause is debug mode's own invisibility toggle. If you see it, before reloading, run `print(getPlayer():isInvisible())` in the debug console and report the result and what you were doing.
- Other health overhauls (anything that replaces bleeding, infection or the health panel) will likely conflict.
- **Take A Bath And Shower** is supported: a bath or shower washes sun block off (`DanTraits_Bathing.lua`), Sensory Issues calms down three times as fast in a bath and twice in a shower (and being wet from it does not count against you), and laundry's hand-wash floors apply to the clothes you bathe in (the same file); both do nothing when it is not loaded. Its gradual body wash is what Germaphobe reads anyway, a wet body cools Multiple Sclerosis as it should, a hot bath warms an MS character (by design on both sides), and its bath salts keep their effects (a calming salt is the game's beta-blocker effect, a happy one its antidepressant effect, which an MDD character's own regimen overrides).
- **Project A-Life** (NPCs on zombie bodies) is supported: its NPCs and its zombie steering leave a Hallucinations phantom alone (`DanTraits_ALife.lua`, which does nothing when A-Life is not loaded). NPC wounds are ordinary wounds, so blood, infection and wound care apply to them, and an NPC's blow, shot or graze counts as a weapon hit for Concussion and Brittle (the game itself does not report those). A house alarm no longer floods the error log through A-Life's NPC hearing (the alarm is not a game object, so it reaches A-Life as a sound with no source).
- **Bandits** (and its add-ons) is supported: bandits, hostile or friendly, leave a Hallucinations phantom alone, and a bandit's hit counts as a weapon hit for Concussion (all of its damage on the head for a head hit) and Brittle (`DanTraits_Bandits.lua`, which does nothing when Bandits is not loaded). A bandit's head shot knocks a helmet off before the concussion roll, so the helmet does not help against that one.
- **This Is Your Life** (the in-world character creator) is supported: its Personnel Assessment folder offers In Their 30s, shows only the ages until one is picked, hides the traits the chosen age cannot take, writes the age's extra cost under a trait's own, and holds Approve and Continue until an age is chosen, like the vanilla screen (`DanTraits_TIYL.lua`, which does nothing when This Is Your Life is not loaded). Its Quick Start and poster stories skip the trait screen, so those characters get the Default Age sandbox setting. Turning off its "Enable Custom Profession Screens" option brings back the vanilla trait screen, which works as normal.
