# Review 2026-11-04: the mod as a whole

A step back after the catchable illnesses landed, the last of the two "next big items" from
2026-10-29. Five read-only surveys by area (health; mind and habit; everyday life; Age, starts, compat,
UI and tooling; the sibling mods), the headline claims re-traced before they were written down here.
`docs/architecture.md` is the map this review refers to. Nothing was changed. Baseline 113/113,
check_drivers 86/86, check_wraps 107 targets clean. Line numbers are from the working tree today
and will drift.

## A. Where it stands

- **Feature complete, by the user's own measure.** The backlog's "next big items" (illnesses, then Age
  v2) are both built. What is left in the backlog is small, and the big open design (multiplayer) is a
  different kind of work. The natural next phase is consolidation, not features.
- **1.5.2 is built and deployed but not committed or uploaded:** 77 modified files and 43 untracked,
  among them `DanTraits_Fever.lua`, `DanTraits_Illness.lua`, `DanTraits_Swarmed.lua`, six item
  textures, two trait icons, three illness plans and twenty test files. The whole illness batch is one
  uncommitted change.
- **Four stale facts a player would meet in 1.5.2** (confirmed): `VitalityChallenges_Dev.lua:18` has
  `local TEST = true` and is committed that way (low population, stocked studio, a free van ship with
  the mod); `DanTraits_GuideData.lua:350` says `whatsNew.version = "1.5.0"` while `mod.info` is 1.5.2,
  so the What's New dialog shows the old notes under the new title; `mod.info`'s description names
  "four crippled starts (A Really Bad Day, Wounded, Robbed, Hunted)" and omits Swarmed and Hunted by
  Sprinters; `plans/backlog.md` still lists illnesses and Age v2 as the next big items and marks
  Laundry, Strain, DTEM and the Collection checklist "uncommitted" though they are committed.

## B. What is solid

The core is the best part of the mod and it shows in the numbers: 86 clock registrations, 214 hook
subscriptions, 71 wraps and 16 ready steps all go through five seams in one file, every one under a
guard that fails loudly once and strictly in the tests. Specifically:

- **The order table is enforced** (`check_drivers.py`), so "who sees whose floors this minute" is
  written down and cannot rot.
- **The frame set** turned a 17-call-a-frame cost into nothing for a character with nothing happening,
  and the `opts.active` / `opts.trait` / `opts.clear` triple is a clean way to say when a system needs
  to run and how it stands down.
- **The delta pipeline** is the right answer to "scale the game's rise without seeing each other's
  writes"; seven stats run through it and the subscribers multiply.
- **One owner for the shared things**: Fever is the only writer of SICKNESS (Illness and Infection are
  sources); Meds is the one drug table and every medicated condition reads it; Faint is the one
  pass-out; Food is the one classifier; Moodles.lua reads and never writes; XpFactor is the one XP
  record.
- **Age reaches two dozen systems without touching their files**, through ~21 named hooks, and
  returns nil in the 30s so every hook is a no-op without branching. Positives is nine traits of pure
  subscriptions. This is the hook seam doing exactly what it was for.
- **The adapters do nothing without their target** and say so in the same words; the tooling checks
  the game's method names, the wrapped methods and Kahlua's limits against the installed game.
- **The tests**: 113 files, a harness that fakes enough of the game to fire events and assert on the
  player, and a STRICT mode so a broken hook fails a test instead of leaving a value alone.

## C. What is uneven: two generations of files

The seams were built in October; about a third of the files predate them or were written beside them
and still talk to the game directly. The result is the same thing done two ways across the tree. None
of these is a bug on its own. Together they are what makes the mod harder to change than its design
deserves, and they are what a multiplayer port would trip over first.

1. **Lifecycle events outside core.** Core says it registers the only `OnCreatePlayer` handler; six
   other files do too: `DanTraits_Wounded.lua:130`, `DanTraits_Hunted.lua:207`,
   `DanTraits_Swarmed.lua:224`, `DanTraits_BadDay.lua:334`, `DanTraits_Reading.lua:734`,
   `client/DanTraits_HealthPanel.lua:476`. Only Robbed of the five starts uses `DanTraits_OnPlayerReady`;
   the others miss its re-run for every local player, and Robbed and Bad Day hard-code
   `getSpecificPlayer(0)` in their world steps (split-screen player 2 gets no fire and no kit). Faint
   runs on `Events.OnTick` where a frame system with `opts.active` is the sanctioned shape.
   Thirty-three `Events.OnGameStart.Add(...)` handlers exist only to call `DanTraits_Wrap` after the
   class is sure to exist, four lines each; five files repeat the boot trio
   (OnGameBoot + OnMainMenuEnter + OnGameStart) to append a line to a trait description.
2. **Two idioms for the one mod-data table.** `DanTraits_Data(player)` in the clock functions, raw
   `player:getModData().DanTraits` in the public getters: 66 raw reads in 25 files (Smoker 6, Migraine 5,
   Fever 4, Arthritis 4, ...). The raw form skips the migration the accessor guarantees.
3. **Cross-file keys read without a getter.** Strain reads eight other files' keys
   (`alcStage`, `ckGlow`, `ftPumpMin`, `hmAtHome`, `hoActive`, `infStage`, `lnMoodle`, `mddEpisode`);
   Heart reads Smoker's `nicMeter` and Sleep's `slCaffeineHours`; Asthma reads `nicLungs`; Tremor
   `cafLevel`; Gout `ckDiet`; Brittle `vitExercise`; Spoons `slNightMin`, `slNightDark`,
   `vitNightHours`; Diabetes `mddEpisode`; Cooking re-derives Home's Homebody multiplier from
   `d.hmAtHome` instead of asking Home. An owner that renames a key silently zeroes a dependent
   condition and no test notices. Anemia's `DanTraits_IronDeficit` is the pattern that should hold
   everywhere.
4. **The same pair of functions in two files.** `holdAnger` / `setAngerFloor` in
   `DanTraits_Dependent.lua:97-122` and `DanTraits_Smoker.lua:225-257`: two mod-data keys and two frame
   systems holding the one ANGER stat, unaware of each other. The starts share `partOf`
   (Wounded = Robbed), `houseOf` (Robbed = Swarmed), `multiplayer()` (Hunted = Swarmed), the
   worn-clothes strip (Robbed ~ Bad Day) and a once-gate each. Eight files define a local
   `sandboxOn()` over `DanTraits_SandboxOn("X")`; six define `xxData(player)` over `DanTraits_Data`;
   AgeRules and VanillaPrices each have a boot-time description appender; the six adapters each
   reinvent detect-and-install (six detection idioms, two install timings, two wrap styles).
5. **Core knows one trait by name.** `DanTraits.lua:929` and `:996` call `DanTraits_MddOnEat` and
   `DanTraits_MddOnPill` directly while every other trait subscribes to the `eat` and `pill` hooks.
   ADHD registers a core delta pipeline (`boredomRise`, `DanTraits_ADHD.lua:141`) that AgeWants also
   subscribes to, so AgeWants depends on ADHD's file existing; Util's comment still says core registers
   three pipelines (it registers six).
6. **Dice.** `ZombRand(100) >= chance`, `ZombRand(10000) < chance * 100` and `list[ZombRand(#list)+1]`
   in some fifteen files (Brittle and Arthritis use no `DanTraits_Roll` at all) where
   `DanTraits_Roll` / `RollPercent` / `RandRange` exist; there is no pick helper. The backlog has had
   this row since 2026-10-02.
7. **Reading is an island.** Carried over from Dan's Vanilla Fixes with its own event wiring
   (`DanTraits_Reading.lua:732-734`), its own player loop with no dead check, its own sandbox reader
   (`R.opt`), its own dice, and no `require "DanTraits"` though it calls `DanTraits_WorldHours`. It is
   the one everyday system invisible to the dashboard's attribution, the guard log and STRICT.
8. **Trait gating, old and new.** The Age 2.0 conditions use `{ trait =, clear = }` so a trait removed
   mid-game clears its keys; Epilepsy, Hemophilia, Anemia, Diabetes, Heart and Asthma check `hasTrait`
   inside and hand-clear.
9. **Moodles.lua mixes three level conventions** (a precomputed `d.xxMoodle`; the owner's
   `DanTraits_XxxMoodle(player, d)`; raw value tiered by a table that lives in Moodles.lua), and nine
   legacy moodles are still fed by their owners through `DanTraits_BadMoodle` (Vitality calls
   `MF.getMoodle` directly for one).
10. **Shared code showing text itself.** `DanTraits_ReadingAction.lua:210-223` and
    `DanTraits_LaundryWash.lua:59-64` call `HaloTextHelper` instead of `DanTraits_Notify`, so those
    lines never reach the story event or the companion app. Bathing hand-wraps a Take A Bath And
    Shower function; A-Life and Bandits hand-replace plain functions because `DanTraits_Wrap` assumes a
    method receiver. SleepMenu defines three timed actions in `client/` where every other action lives
    in `shared/TimedActions` (the server needs them).
11. **Vitality's Thriving adds XP raw** (`DanTraits_Vitality.lua:465-476`: a second `AddXP` with half
    the amount, re-entry guarded) where Fitness, Strain, Age and ADHD multiply through
    `DanTraits_XpFactor`. Core's XP record was written to expect that inner event, so it is not a
    double-count; it is the one factor that takes a different road and that Attrib cannot credit.
12. **Headers that no longer match the code**: Clotting ("the menu in DanTraits_Client.lua"), Faint
    ("used by shock, concussion and Fear of Blood": ten callers now), Meds (five daily drugs: 27 ids),
    Infection's subscriber list, Epilepsy's half-deleted mod-data line, Home's `hmNights` listed with
    two meanings, Strain's "API for every system that feeds it" (two callers), Caffeine's reader list,
    Client's header. Diabetes, Asthma and Brittle have their real header after the first locals, which
    breaks the `^--` convention the tooling reads. About a dozen exported globals have no caller in
    the Lua, the tests or the companion (`DanTraits_FeverCompose`, `FeverSources`, `HayFeverLoad`,
    `IllnessOrder` and the other Illness table exports, ...).
13. **The sibling mods each re-roll the small things**: a guard (Challenges' `guard` and two identical
    `try` copies; Base Unloader has 86 bare pcalls), a halo (Base Unloader `say`, Challenges `halo`
    and `note`, five inline `HaloTextHelper` calls), a sandbox read with default (three copies, two of
    them inside Dan's Vanilla Fixes), a clamp, a clock, and three wrap styles, two of which
    (Map Legend's four, Base Unloader's two) have no flag and double-wrap on a Lua reload.
    `DanShared_Tooltip.lua` shows the model that works: a byte-identical copy per mod, a global guard,
    deploy.py refusing drift, a test loading both.
14. **Release bookkeeping.** CHANGELOG has no "unreleased" convention (1.5.2 is dated and headed but
    not shipped); the README promises Base Unloader "its own Workshop item" but there is no
    `workshop/baseunloader` and `deploy.py --workshop BaseUnloader` exits; Dan's Vanilla Fixes at 1.2.0
    has no page; README has no section for Map Legend, Cartridges or Challenges; Cartridges' only tests
    are the five untracked files in its `tools/`.
