# Code review, bug hunt and synergy map, 2026-10-16

Scope: every Lua file in both mods (DanTraits 26,000 lines across 59 shared, 13 client, 5 timed-action and 3 server files; Dan's Vanilla Fixes 8 files), the scripts, sandbox options, translations, mod.info, deploy.py and the offline tests. Working tree on `master` at b5ba8c7 plus the uncommitted Reading move. Baseline: `python tests/run_tests.py` passes 76/76; `lint_kahlua.py` 0 errors, 1 warning (Diabetes at 50 upvalues); `check_api.py` 0 problems.

Since the 2026-10-08 review (`refactor-review-2026-10-08.md`): its Tier 1 and Tier 2 (items 1-10) are applied. Items 11-26 and the tidy-ups are still open and are not repeated here; nothing in them has turned into a correctness problem, but 14 (drug registry), 17 (Wrap re-apply) and 18 (pill menus) have grown more urgent and are named below where they bite.

Six reviewers read the files by cluster; every finding below marked **confirmed** I then traced myself in the working tree (and, for the vehicle and drink-action ones, in the installed B42 Lua). "Plausible" means the code path is real but the game-side fact is unverified offline.

## Verdict

The architecture holds up: the driver order, the hook registry, the delta pipeline, Guard, SetTrait and StartingKit are doing their jobs, and the consistency pass found nothing missing or misplaced from yesterday's Reading move (keys, items, sandbox options and deploy are all consistent). The bugs are almost all at the seams where one system reads another, which is also where the unused synergies are. One root cause, the drink action firing its hook on every tick, explains three symptoms. The biggest single risk is the unplayed code: Laundry has the most findings and the most multiplayer-shaped questions.

## A. Bugs, ranked

### A1. Fix before the Saturday upload

1. **The `drink` hook fires on every tick of a drink, not once per drink.** `DanTraits_Diabetes.lua:596-618` wraps `ISDrinkFluidAction:updateEat`, and vanilla calls `updateEat(jobDelta)` from `update()` every tick and `updateEat(1)` from `complete()` (B42 `shared/TimedActions/ISDrinkFluidAction.lua:29,45,105`). The same holds for Alcohol's `alcoholDrunk` wrap (`DanTraits_Alcohol.lua:90-99`), which passes no amount at all. Three subscribers were written as if they get one call per drink. **Confirmed.**
   - *Delicate Stomach* (`DanTraits_AgeTraits.lua:510-517`): +10 FOOD_SICKNESS per tick on a drink with hunger over 0.5. One beer is Nauseous within about ten ticks.
   - *Caffeine to Sleep* (`DanTraits_Caffeine.lua:146-153` then `DanTraits_Sleep.lua:120-126` and `DanTraits_SpoonMask`): Sleep takes `max(existing, 6h * amount/80)` of each sip, so a mug of coffee from a cup gives minutes of caffeine hours, not six; only the eaten HotDrink item (600 units at once) gets the documented six. `test_sleep` 9 calls `SleepOnCaffeine(100)` directly, which is why it passes.
   - *Lactose* (`DanTraits_Lactose.lua:68-95`): `addDose` notifies on every call, so a glass of milk is dozens of halos and dozens of OnStoryEvent lines for PZ Chronicle.
   - Fix at the root: in Diabetes' wrap, accumulate litres per action object and run `drink` (and `alcoholDrunk`, with litres) once when the action completes or stops, or when the fluid name changes. Then Delicate Stomach doses by litres, Caffeine's per-dose maths stays, Lactose notifies when `lacPending` crosses zero. Add a test that drives the wrap with ten `updateEat(0.1)` calls and one `updateEat(1)`.

2. **A fainting driver's car never stops.** `DanTraits_Faint.lua:161-167` sends `sendClientCommand(player, "vehicle", "shutOff", {})` and only calls `car:shutOff()` if that *throws*. The command has no handler in B42: vanilla's own `ISExitVehicle.lua:30,54` has that exact line commented out, and `ISShutOffVehicleEngine.lua:30` calls `vehicle:shutOff()` directly. `pcall` succeeds, the fallback never runs, the car drives on with a blacked-out driver. The header already says the vehicle branch is untried, and there is no `test_faint.lua`. Fix: call `car:shutOff()` first. **Confirmed.**

3. **Antibiotics taken before an infection shows are thrown away, and can turn into a relapse.** `DanTraits_Infection.lua:340-361`: while a wound is only contaminated (`rec.inc`), `maxL == 0`, `S == 0`, so neither branch sets `infActive`, and line 361 sets `infDoses = 0` every minute. Start a course on a dirty bite, the infection shows a day later with the count at 0, one more dose clears it, `infDoses` is 1, so `infUnfinished` and a 50% relapse roll 24 hours later. The player did the right thing and is punished for it. `test_infection` 7 infects first and doses after, so the path is unpinned. Fix: do not reset `infDoses` while any part is contaminated (or count doses taken during incubation toward the course). **Confirmed.**

4. **A bandage on a hemophiliac soaks at the unhooked bleed rate.** `DanTraits_WoundCare.lua:247-249` reads `DanTraits_BloodPartRate`, which is Blood's raw `partRate` (`DanTraits_Blood.lua:153`); the `bloodBleed` hooks (Hemophilia x2.5, Good Clotter x0.75) are applied only inside `bleedMinute` (`:163`). So the dressing-change pressure the Hemophilia header promises never comes, and Good Clotter's bandages do not last longer. `test_woundcare` stubs `BloodPartRate` to a constant. Fix: export a hooked per-part rate from Blood and keep the raw one private. **Confirmed.**

5. **The food classifier misfires on real B42 names.** Run over all 722 `base:food` items (`scratchpad/food_tags.txt` has the output): `Base.Chamomile`/`ChamomileDried` are **meat** ("ham"), so a Vegetarian refuses chamomile tea; `Base.TurkeyEgg` is meat + egg; `Base.CandyGummyfish` is meat with full iron credit for an anaemic; `Base.CannedMushroomSoupOpen`, `TinnedSoupOpen`, `BagelPoppy`, `PoppyPods`, `PoppySeed` are **junk** ("pop"), so Delicate Stomach's junk nausea and Vitality's diet grade fire on opened canned soup; `JellyBeans` and `ChocolateCoveredCoffeeBeans` are greens. The other way: seven B42 `FoodType = Candy` items carry no junk tag because `FOOD_TYPES` (`DanTraits_Food.lua:162`) maps only meat/egg/greens. Fix: `FOOD_SAFE` entries for the six misses, and map the B42 `Candy`/`Chocolate` food types to junk (a cheaper and more reliable signal than the name list). `test_food.lua` tests invented names; a test over the real list would pin it. **Confirmed.**

6. **Diabetes counts any low endurance as exercise.** `DanTraits_Diabetes.lua:506` `if moving or endurance < DIA_EXERT_ENDURANCE then g = g - DIA_EXERCISE_DROP`. Endurance under 0.6 from an Asthma attack, a Heart angina regen cut, Blood, Anemia or Spoons at the wall all count. A Type 1 with insulin on board who has an asthma attack loses 30 mg/dL an hour on top of the insulin for as long as endurance stays low: a low, panic, more drain. Asthma itself requires "still being spent" (`:159-161`). Use the same spent-this-minute test. **Confirmed.**

7. **Heart counts its own angina surcharge, and other systems' drains, as exertion.** `DanTraits_Heart.lua:233` `spent = hcEndPrev - endurance` is every endurance drop in the minute, including the frame handler's own 1.5x angina surcharge (`:318-322`, which does `DeltaRemember` for the regen pipeline but never adjusts `hcEndPrev`). During chest pain a swing feeds strain at 1.5x, which deepens the angina: a loop. An Asthma attack at rest (0.15/min x HC_SPEND 0.6) is chest pain in about eleven minutes of sitting still. Fix: subtract `extra` from `hcEndPrev` in the frame handler, and decide whether asthma-strains-the-heart is wanted (if so, say it in the header). **Confirmed.**

8. **Epilepsy: an aura that starts asleep seizes an awake player with no warning.** `DanTraits_Epilepsy.lua:97-103` sets `epAuraMin` and returns before the notice and the 30-second real-time floor when asleep. Anything that wakes the player inside the window (Asthma's gasp, Sleep's light wake, the alarm) runs the countdown out at `:148-152` and `seize()` takes the awake branch: no notice, weapon dropped, knock, deep pass-out. Also `:156` rolls again during the 60-minute aftermath, when a head knock multiplies the rate x6. Fix: notify on the first awake minute with `epAuraMin` set; skip the roll while `epAfterMin > 0`. **Confirmed.**

9. **Laundry's machine wash is marked while the machine is merely running, and a stacked washer-dryer is not a washer.** `DanTraits_Laundry.lua:639-653` sets `s.mw` on every ten-minute tick an activated washer holds the garment; `L.sight` then applies the wash. Load, start, pull the clothes straight out: odour 0, musty cleared, "washed once", a wear roll, on garments still at full blood. And `runningWasher`/`isWasher` (`:624-636`) know only `IsoClothingWasher` and `IsoCombinationWasherDryer`; the washer client happily loads an `IsoStackedWasherDryer` (`DanTraits_LaundryWasher.lua:607-613`), whose cycle then zeroes blood and dirt without any of the mod's bookkeeping, and loses a set-in stain the plan says a machine must not clear. Fix: apply `mw` only when the garment reads blood 0, dirt 0 and wetness 100; add the stacked class. **Confirmed.**

10. **Laundry's tooltip strip draws on top of the shared strip.** `client/DanTraits_LaundryClient.lua:305-327` keeps its own `ISToolTipInv:render` wrap and `drawLines` at `top = self.height`; `DanShared_Tooltip.lua` draws at the same place. With DVF's Item Info on, every garment gets a "Covers:" line, so any garment with a laundry line shows two boxes painted over each other. Fix: delete the wrap, register through `DanShared_Tooltip.add("laundry", 22, ...)` (the `laundryTooltip` hook already returns the same `{text, bad}` shape). **Confirmed.**

11. **Reading: a comfort re-read overwrites the finish record's verdict, and two "no glasses" rules stack.** `DanTraits_Reading.lua:496-500` recomputes the verdict with `level = 0` on a re-read, so a book recorded "loved" becomes "great", and the tooltip and journal change. Keep the record's verdict when `reread`. Separately, `DanTraits_ReadingHooks.lua:243-253,278-283` adds +10%/+15% per page to every 40s/50s character not wearing exactly `Base.Glasses_Reading`, while the paid Reading Glasses trait (`DanTraits_AgeTraits.lua:165-197`) adds x1.5 unless any of five glasses types is worn; both multiply (1.725 for a 50s trait-holder with no glasses) and disagree about prescription glasses. One rule: ReadingHooks reads the exported `DanTraits_NeedsGlasses`. **Confirmed.**

12. **Vitality switched off leaves a sleep debt three systems keep reading.** `DanTraits_Vitality.lua:266-275` gives back the carry kilo and zeroes the moodles but leaves `vitSleepDebt`; `DanTraits_SleepDebt` (`:261-264`) does not check the option, unlike `VitalityEffect`. MDD (episode chance x2), Hallucinations (+0.25) and Migraine's sleep trigger read the stale debt for the rest of the save. With Vitality off nothing runs `nightQuality`, so Sleep's `slNightDark`/`slNightMin` grow forever. Fix: the off branch clears the sleep keys; Sleep resets when the character wakes with no scorer. **Confirmed.**

13. **A cancelled meal reaches every eat reader at 0%, and MDD ignores the fraction.** `DanTraits.lua:572-578` passes `progress` 0 through; vanilla's `stop()` calls `eat(item, jobDelta)` on every cancel. Diabetes, Vitality, Gluten, Anemia, Caffeine, Smoker scale by the fraction and no-op; `DanTraits_MddOnEat` (`DanTraits_MDD.lua:147-152`) takes no fraction, so starting any comfort food and cancelling gives 180 minutes of relief. Fix: return early in core when progress is 0; pass the fraction to MddOnEat. **Confirmed.**

**Kahlua headroom.** Diabetes' minute handler is at 50 upvalues, Vitality's at 46, Smoker's at 41; the cap is 60. Every new constant a handler touches costs one. The backlog's constants-table row applies to all three now.

**Test gaps that let the above hide.** Nothing drives the drink wrap per tick (1). No `test_faint.lua` (2). `test_infection` never doses before infecting (3). `test_woundcare` stubs the bleed rate flat (4). `test_food` uses invented names (5). ## D. Synergies not yet used

The hook map says where the seams already are. Every hook defined has a runner and a registrant, but several systems expose state that nothing reads, and several classic pairings go through nothing but the vanilla stat. Grouped by cost.

### One hook or one line away

1. **Smoke on your clothes.** `laundryOdour` is run by Laundry and registered only by LaundryTraits. Smoker registers it: a smoker's worn layers go stale faster, and Cool Customer, Germaphobe and Sensitive Skin react to the smell. Dependent could add the same for a heavy drinker's clothes.
2. **The post-concussion headache feeds Migraine.** `DanTraits_ConcussionLateStrength` has no reader; Migraine weights only `ConcussionStrength`, so the headache that outlasts the score (the one the health panel shows) never triggers an attack. One term in `migraineParts`. Hallucinations' `schizoDown` is the other natural reader.
3. **Hangover and Dehydration know each other.** Dehydration reads only the thirst moodle; Hangover only adds thirst. A `hangoverSeverity` hook from Dehydration (dehydrated means a worse hangover) and Hangover adding to `dhLoad` closes the loop. Migraine reads raw thirst, not the hours-long `dhLoad`; swap the read.
4. **Heart reads Blood, Anemia and fever.** Heart's strain rate scales with smoking, caffeine, Vitality and age, but not with low blood volume, low cells or a fever, the three things that make a real heart race. Three `DanTraits_Strength` reads in `strainRate`. Cold is the fourth: Heart adds strain only outside under 5 C, so a soaked body indoors builds nothing; read `TEMPERATURE` the way MS and Migraine do.
5. **Arthritis and Old Injury feel the storm coming.** Migraine already has `stormComing()`; Old Bones predicts tomorrow's weather. Move the storm reader to Util and give Arthritis and Old Injury a flare in the twelve hours before rain. Old Bones then has a mechanical reason to exist beside the warning.
6. **Morning stiffness.** Arthritis registers no `sleepWake`; the first half hour after waking is the classic worst time, eased by warmth and a hot drink (Caffeine's `drink` hook names the fluid). One registration.
7. **Nocturnal asthma, nocturnal hypo.** Asthma wakes you gasping at tier 3 but registers no `nightQuality`, so a mild irritation costs nothing at night; Diabetes registers neither `sleepWake` nor `nightQuality`, so a low during sleep is silent until morning. A night low should wake you sweating and cost the night; both are one registration each.
8. **Intolerances and the diet grade.** `foodGrade` is run by Vitality and registered only by Positives. An intolerant character's bread or cheese scores "fresh, cooked" and then flares. Gluten and Lactose register it (and Vegetarian, for the meat it refuses, so a vegetarian diet is scored on what they eat).
9. **Coffee with your iron.** Anemia's eat hook credits iron; Caffeine's level is in mod data. Tea or coffee with the meal halves absorption. One read of `cafLevel` at eat time, and a real reason to time the pills.
10. **Tinnitus is read by nobody.** `tnRinging` could feed Migraine (a noise term), Sleep's `nightQuality` (ringing in a silent room; it already lightens sleep through `sleepWake`) and Schizophrenia (whispers are likelier while the ears ring). Its input could widen too: the guarded `OnWorldSound` seam in `DanTraits_ALife.lua` already hears alarms, so bandit and A-Life gunfire and explosions could count, which is the only way Tinnitus matters for a player who does not shoot.

### Needs a little state or a design decision

11. **Brittle gets a bone score.** Today one 20% roll. A score fed by dairy and greens through the eat hook (so Brittle plus Lactose Intolerant is a real combination: no cheap calcium), by exercise regularity (Gym Regular, Vitality's exercise score) and by age (the `brittleChance` hook already has Age on it) turns Brittle into something you manage rather than suffer. The eat and regularity reads both exist.
12. **Home as a mood lever for Spiraling and Spoons.** MDD's episode chance and recovery read exercise and outdoors; Home's calm runs at order 11 and is floored straight back by MDD at 40 (by design). Giving MDD a `HomeStrength` relief term makes Homebody plus Spiraling "home is the comfort" and Nomad plus Spiraling "the road is". Spoons' rest rule could refill a little faster at home (Homebody plus MS). Both read an export that already exists.
13. **Spoons for more than MS.** Spoons is "a pool with registered users" and only MS registers. Spiraling episodes (depression fatigue) and a deep Anemia deficit are the two spoon-theory conditions that fit; `SpoonsUse` is the registration. Check first that a washboard session spends rather than rests (`resting()` is "not moving, not heavy, not in a vehicle"; hand-washing spends vanilla endurance, so `met` should see it, but it is unplayed).
14. **Reading at bedtime, reading in an attack.** A Good Read moodle active when you fall asleep is a `nightQuality` bonus (a chapter before bed). A migraine attack with blur (`migBlur`) should refuse or slow reading the way the too-dim check does; ReadingHooks already reads Concussion, so the seam is there. A good book could also ease Anger's curses (Anger only slows reading today).
15. **Sensitive Skin and the sun.** SensitiveSkin runs at 41 but reads only laundry; a sensitive skin burning faster (`sbBurn` x1.3) and cracking from bleach (the `AsthmaFumes` seam LaundryTraits already uses for the airway) makes the trait more than an itch.
16. **Knox reads Vitality.** The Resilient survival roll is a flat sandbox number; weighting it by `VitalityEffect` (a Run Down body is less likely to beat it) gives Vitality a stake in the one roll everyone cares about.
17. **A Really Bad Day draws from the new systems.** It starts naked, drunk, soaked, sick and cut; a hangover already follows from drunk. With Laundry and Home in, "the only clothes are a musty bag next door" and "no bed you have slept in" are free flavour, and Sunburn makes "and it is July" a fourth day-one problem.
