| Catchable illnesses, no trait needed (design talked through 2026-10-13): seven, so viral vs bacterial matters. Viral: common cold (wet and cold, sleep debt, contact; sneeze and cough noise; many strains; replaces vanilla cold), seasonal flu (Nov-Mar; fever, aches, fatigue; one strain a winter; dangerous for Elderly, Asthma, Heart, Smoker, Diabetes), stomach bug (dirty hands then eating; vomiting and thirst; hand washing counters it). Bacterial: food poisoning (raw or rotten food, 6-24 h incubation, replaces vanilla food sickness; dehydration kills), waterborne gut infection (untreated river, lake, rain water; weeks of thirst and weight loss until antibiotics; boiling matters), strep (often after a cold; antibiotics clear it), pneumonia (secondary to flu or cold if you keep exerting, stay wet, or are a smoker, asthmatic or elderly; endurance cap, high fever, health drain; antibiotics required). Tetanus only as a sandbox-off option, or skipped.
