# Review 2026-10-30: the Saturday upload (1.5.0)

The last pass before the 2026-10-31 Workshop upload. Four reviewers by area (the three
crippled starts; the Guide, What's New, DTEM and the review refactors in a2c4d24/283e3ad;
dca1ede and The Last Broadcast; a fact-check of every number in the Steam notes against the
code), every finding re-traced here before it was fixed. Baseline 106/106, lint clean.

## A. Fixed

1. **Wounded rolled the Knox infection on about a third of starts.** `BodyPart.setCut(b)` is
   `setCut(b, true)` and the `true` is `generateZombieInfection(25)`; `setScratched(b, true)`
   rolls 7. One cut and two scratches: 1 - 0.75 x 0.93 x 0.93 = 35% of Wounded characters would
   have been dying of the virus from the first minute. Every cut and scratch the mod makes now
   passes `false`: Wounded, **A Really Bad Day** (pre-existing: a quarter of Bad Day starts since
   the trait was written), the Blood console `wound` command and Vitality Challenges' `VC.cut`.
   Confirmed from the jar (both two-argument signatures exist).
2. **Hunted spawned nothing in singleplayer.** Zombies are made on loaded squares only and
   IsoChunkMap keeps 13 chunks of 8 tiles a side (about 50 tiles around the player); every
   distance tried (200 down to 100, the dawn packs 150 down to 100) was unloaded, so after 30
   quiet minutes the horde was marked loose with no horde, the house pulled with a world sound
   for four hours anyway, and the dawn packs never came. Only the helicopter worked. The
   distance lists now run down to 40, so the farthest loaded spot (50-65 tiles, beyond sight)
   is used, and the release moves from 1.5 to 2.75 hours so they still arrive around hour three
   (a shambler covers 50 tiles in a quarter of an hour; the notice is the warning). The test
   has a 52-tile loaded-area case.
3. **Hunted's dawn packs were a morning late.** `getNightsSurvived` increments at 07:00, so at
   06:00 of the first morning it is 0 and `day >= 1` fired on mornings 2, 3 and 4. Morning n is
   nights + 1; the first three mornings once the horde is loose (a 05:00 start is not hunted at
   06:00 before anything else has happened).
4. **Robbed destroyed the medical starting kits.** Core's one OnCreatePlayer handler runs the
   ready steps (kits at order 40) before the trait files' own handlers, and Robbed's
   `getInventory():clear()` then took the insulin, meter and syringe, the inhaler, the
   anticonvulsants, the MS and heart drugs, the Pill Caddy; the kit flags were set, so the
   OnGameStart re-run never regranted. A Robbed Type 1 diabetic died within Untreated Diabetes
   Days. The stripping is now a ready step at order 35, before the kits. Pinned in the test.
5. **Robbed's house footprint reached the neighbour.** `BuildingDef.x2/y2` are one past the last
   tile, so in a row house the loops and the fill guard emptied the containers on the far side of
   the party wall and kept them empty for 48 hours. A square now counts only when its room's
   building definition starts where the house's does (falls back to rectangle + room when the
   building cannot be read). Tested with a party-wall case.
6. **Robbed's rag was described as "about an hour's" bandage.** With the bullet in, the blood
   through it (deep-wound rate x0.35) soaks 1.5 life in about two minutes (WoundCare: 1.5 life
   per 1% through); spent, it still halves the bleed, so left alone: pale in about 20 minutes,
   shock about 50, dead about an hour. Code unchanged; the comment, README and Steam line now
   say that (the hour is still the honest headline).
7. **The Medication window outlived its character.** On a death and new character in one
   session the old window was forgotten, not removed, and went on drawing the dead character's
   list every frame. Taken down at OnCreatePlayer.
8. **Guide pictures were asked of the texture loader on every redraw** (none ship). A miss is
   remembered per page.
9. **The Last Broadcast: the dial silenced every minute, the evening settled from 20:00 on.**
   Vanilla scripts picked up between the hourly silencings played for up to 59 minutes; a stall
   during sleep that skipped the hour `== 20` lost the evening (no miss, no loss, no failure).

## B. Confirmed, not changed

- Every number in the Steam notes matches the code (costs, Burnout's 15% / 2000 kills / 90 days,
  adrenaline's 1 in 4 and 40 minutes, fabric protector's five washes, Newbie Gains' 30% and three
  levels, the quiz's 40 questions, DTEM's six and four, the Fixed bullets exist). "Up to twice as
  often" for the conditions under Burnout is x2 at Burnt Out, x2.33 at Breaking Point; left.
- `DanTraits_XpFactor`'s `elseif extra > 0` fallback (no Age loaded) would recurse through the
  listeners; unreachable, Age is always loaded. Noted for the day it is not.
- `DanTraits_GreyOut` takes the inventory menu's tooltip pool for world menus too; the two menus
  are never open together. Cosmetic.
- The frame snapshot is read before the drivers, so a sprint Heart's angina blocks still counts
  as a sprint for WoundCare's movement and Blood's cant-sprint moodle that frame. Negligible.
- `state.beaconTick` in The Last Broadcast counts cumulative on-air minutes, so the station
  sound is every five on-air minutes across shows, not per show. Harmless.
- The LBMW `register = false` entry still lands in every save's `dynamicRadio` mod data (name,
  frequency and uuid only).
- The refactors in a2c4d24 preserve behaviour at every call site (Diabetes constant-for-constant,
  Util helpers argument-for-argument, the ready list in the old order and idempotent for the
  OnGameStart re-run); the Guide and What's New files follow the house patterns; DTEM is a no-op
  without the mod and its table matches the script.
