The second pass before the 2026-10-31 Workshop upload: everything the user built after the
2026-10-30 review (plans/review-2026-10-30.md), which turned out to be the whole of Age 2.0
(steps 1 to 7 of plans/age-2.0.md: the mod's own Smoker, the vanilla traits left alone, the

## A. Fixed

1. **Smoker withdrawal double-counted stress.** The file added `NIC_W_STRESS` and `NIC_W_MOOD`
   every waking minute as "what the game's Java gave its own Smoker's withdrawal". The jar says
   otherwise: `BodyDamage.UpdateBoredom` raises only `NICOTINE_WITHDRAWAL`, and
   `Stats.getNicotineStress()` returns STRESS + NICOTINE_WITHDRAWAL, which `Moodle` reads for the
   Stress moodle for everyone. The mod's stat already showed and counted as stress; the adds put
   real STRESS on top (saturating in about 20 waking hours at full withdrawal, then feeding
   Burnout, MDD, Migraines). Both adds and constants removed; the test pins zero.
2. **A shown vanilla Smoker could end up with both Smokers.** With Show Vanilla Smoker on, a
   vanilla Smoker who smokes built the habit meter as a non-smoker and `becomeSmoker` granted ours
   after a week (the script exclusion only acts at creation). The gain path now skips a character
   with `base:smoker`.
3. **The wants' day turned over at the start hour, not midnight.** `dayIndex()` was
   `floor(worldHours / 24)`; with the default 09:00 start the night scored at 07:00 saw no
   roll, so the routine compared yesterday's drink with the meal of two days back, and the 40s'
   once-a-day touches reset at 09:00. Now `DanTraits_DayIndex()` (Util, midnight to midnight).
4. **"New ground" lifted and notified on every first visit to any map cell**, itchy or not: a
   20s character driving across fresh country got -10 unhappiness and a halo line each new cell.
   The lift is the want's relief: gated on the want being up. Test updated.
5. **The late sleeper's held tiredness fought the `fatigueRise` pipeline.** The hold set FATIGUE to
   0.62 without `DanTraits_DeltaRemember`, so the next minute the pipeline read the jump as a game
   rise and scaled it: Deep Sleeper wrote it back down and the hold pushed it up again every
   minute for an hour (the Drowsy moodle flickering), Thyroid overshot. Remembered, as Vitality's
   debt already is.
6. **The 50s' meal sliver was not once a day.** `kept()` gated only the 40s; every snack of 0.2
   hunger within the hour of yesterday's main meal paid. Once a day for both bands.
7. **Steady Hands' x0.5 only reached the first item of a drag.** `ISInventoryTransferAction`
   merges the other items of a multi-item move into `queueList` with the time each `new()`
   computed and resets `maxTime` from it, never through `adjustMaxTime`; a Steady Hands character
   dragging twelve stacks had one halved and eleven at full time, where vanilla Dexterous halves
   all twelve. The merged entries are now halved once each after `checkQueueList` (never `new`,
   per the MP rule). Carpal Tunnel's x1.2 on transfers has the same reach and is left (a nit on
   a numb spell, not a headline claim).
8. **The four new drugs had no side-effect or overdose notices.** `DanTraits_Meds` notifies
   `UI_DanTraits_MedSide_<id>` / `MedOver_<id>`; allopurinol, colchicine and levothyroxine had
   neither key and antihistamines no overdose line, so a 3% morning side effect would have
   printed the raw key. Seven keys added.
9. **Antihistamines never spawned in glove boxes**: `GloveBox` is not a B42 procedural list (the
   glove box is a clutter table). Moved to `clutter.GloveBoxItems` as Sunblock has it.
10. **Carpal Tunnel listed three action names that do not exist in this build** (`ISSawLogs`,
    `ISRepairAction`, `ISDigFurrowAction`): dropped; sawing and repairs are `ISHandcraftAction` /
    `ISFixAction`, which are listed.
11. **The Steam notes' point signs.** The four new "New" bullets used the creation screen's sign
    ("Gout (+8)", "Natural (-3)", "Smoker (+5)") while the rest of the page, the FAQ ("minus means
    a flaw") and the README use the other. Corrected: Gout -8, Essential Tremor -3, Underactive
    Thyroid -3, Hay Fever -2, Carpal Tunnel -2, Natural +3, Hot-Headed -3, Restless -3, Smoker -5.
12. **Wording that did not match the code**: Hot-Headed "anger builds half again as fast" (the
    hook scales the irritability a craving holds, rises are untouched) in the Steam note, the
    trait description and the README table; Restless "days at home" (the want counts map cells,
    home has nothing to do with it) in the note and the description; Thyroid "until you find
    levothyroxine" (it starts with a bottle); allopurinol "x0.6 at full effect" (rises x0.4, drain
    x1.6) in CHANGELOG and README; the "what an age can take" note and the What's New line
    omitted Gout and Underactive Thyroid; the Smoker's Athletic exclusion (the script's, as
    vanilla's) named nowhere; CHANGELOG's "a starting bottle" for all five (Carpal Tunnel has no
    drug); "Restless doubles the days, the dip and the lift" (the boredom factor too).
13. **The mod's Smoker had no icon.** The game builds a trait's icon from
    `media/ui/Traits/trait_<key>.png` with a generic fallback; no loose `trait_smoker.png`
    exists in the mod or the game folder (vanilla's is packed). Drawn in `make_icons.py` (a lit
    cigarette), shipped.
14. Small ones: `angerFall` clamped at 0; `d.vanLevelsApplied` set after the `DanTraits_AddLevels`
    check, not before; the Client header comment still said the wrap hides Wakeful.

## B. Confirmed, not changed

- The owned withdrawal stat is safe against the Java: `UpdateBoredom` raises it only under
  `hasTrait(SMOKER)` and never zeroes it; the non-Smoker eat branch adds nausea and hunger only;
  the file's rise stands, the after-hook takes the nausea back.
- Ready-step order and idempotency across the whole batch (retire 20, cache 30, SmokerConvert 35,
  Age 50, VanillaPrices 55, Knox 60, Positives 65, Sleep 70, SteadyHands 75): a 1.4.0 20s with
  Green loses the trait and keeps the levels, `ageApplied` keeps the new -2/-1 off them; a 1.4.0
  Keen Cook keeps its Short Blade level with no double grant; a player-chosen Dexterous / Fast
  Healer / Wakeful is never touched (no flag). A stored Profession Levels (20s) of 0 is read as 0
  (the user's own preset shows 0 on the creation screen: expected until changed).
- Signs on the creation screen: `PointToSpend` wrap `points - extra`, "+6 Vitality" on Resilient
  takes 6; deltas apply with no age chosen; TIYL goes through the same functions. The 0-cost 30s
  and 40s land only in the positive list on both screens.
- The rules table reproduces every old price row but the three the changelog removes, and the old
  age-only table minus Green plus the eight new rows; Hard of Hearing reachable through
  `DanTraits_AgeRuleBands`.
- Every hook default 1, registrants multiply, Age returns nil outside the 20s; `awCells` string
  keys; the sandbox switch gates every tick and hook; split-screen clean.
- Gout: `PartPainAtLeast` is a raise-only floor, so a flare never clobbers a wound's pain and decays
  off by itself; the no-run copy is Bad Back's; flare chance is per minute (≈0.095/day at a full
  meter). Hay Fever months 0-based and right. Carpal's and Steady Hands' `adjustMaxTime` wraps
  chain. Meds declarations match the pattern; kits right; `MedSchedule` lists the two daily ones.
- `convertOldSmoker` converts a vanilla Smoker whose withdrawal stat is above zero (a save made
  with the mod off and loaded now): the test pins it as deliberate.
- Tremor's moodle shows at rest (base 0.4 over tier 0.35): the trait is permanent; left.
- The late sleeper's "before 7" is read at the night's scoring, an hour after waking: "woke before
  about 06:00" in practice. Cosmetic.
- `DanTraits_UngrantFoldIn` would also take a twin a character held before the mod first saw them
  (the old flag was set when `SetTrait` found it already there): unreachable in practice (the
  pairs were excluded or hidden). Noted.
